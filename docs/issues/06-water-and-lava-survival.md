# Water and lava survival: vanilla water physics, a priority-zero escape-water goal, liquid-aware memory and path costs, fall damage, respawn

## Summary

Today the bot cannot tell that it is in water. `physics.clj` is "Full-cube collision only; no fluids" (`src/clojurecraft/physics.clj:2-3`), `:liquid` is in `blocks/passable-types` (`src/clojurecraft/blocks.clj:17`), so a pond is air to the simulator: the bot sinks at land gravity, walks along the bottom, and would drown without ever knowing. There is no `:player/air`, `set-health` only records a number (`src/clojurecraft/game.clj:212`), nothing handles death, and the planner cannot interrupt a running intent (`plan-tick` runs `:plan/intent` to completion before it ever calls `choose` again, `src/clojurecraft/plan.clj:71-81`). The siblings' race logs say drowning is the dominant overworld death and that water-find is the deepest pre-Nether wall. This issue adds vanilla liquid movement as pure code, `:player/in-water?` and friends, water and lava as watched block kinds plus water-trap facts, an `:escape-water` goal at priority 0 with real preemption in the planner, liquid and drop costs for the pathfinder, fall-distance tracking, death and respawn handling, and a pond fixture in `sim` so all of it is tested without a server.

## Why now / what the siblings learned

Both bots paid for water with weeks of race time. The specific traps, so clojurecraft does not repeat them:

1. **The physics engine computed in-water and never published it.** typecraft fixed this in `applyPlayerState`: "Without this, bot.entity.isInWater is stuck at its default (false) forever ... every consumer (water-escape, drowning guard, digging-in-water) was blind" (`/Users/bridger/Developer/mc/upstream/steve/src/lib/typecraft/physics/physics.ts:1201-1204`). In clojurecraft the attribute must be written by `physics/step` itself, like `:player/on-ground?`.
2. **The only lift out of water is the wall impulse, so you escape by pressing into a bank.** `vel.y = config.outOfLiquidImpulse` (0.3) fires only when `isCollidedHorizontally && doesNotCollide(... +0.6 ...)` (`physics.ts:868-877`). ruststeve's port passed the bare offset instead of a position, so "the free-space test ran at the world origin and the out-of-water lift onto a ledge fired only by chance. A swimming bot pressed against a 1-high bank never climbed out" (`/Users/bridger/Developer/mc/upstream/ruststeve/src/physics/physics.rs:924-927`). The memory note `typecraft-water-physics.md` adds: lock onto one bank and hold forward, do not re-pick every tick, never dig the bank you need to collide with.
3. **Heading matters more than swimming.** ruststeve: "`leave_water` drove `forward` in an arbitrary heading → deeper → drowned. FIX: `water_exit_dir` faces the nearest bank (head-air column) before swimming" (`ruststeve/LOOP.md:89`).
4. **"Head in water" tested the wrong block.** `head_in_water` checked feet+1, not the eye at y+1.62, so a surface floater (feet ≈ 61.97, eye in air) "counts as submerged" and `mine_stone` looped for an hour (`ruststeve/CHANGES.md:515-516`, fix at `541-542`).
5. **Survival ran only between steps.** "a bot whose head went under mid-step drowned inside the step" (`CHANGES.md:228`); the fix was a per-tick breath watchdog and a `breath_alarm` that ends any step. steve's answer is structural: `escape_water` is `priority: 0`, `canExecute: (s) => s.inWaterTrap`, `isComplete: (s) => !s.inWaterTrap`, re-evaluated every cycle (`steve/src/lib/steve/steps.ts:39-64`), and the only step allowed to outrank entering a lit portal (`steps.ts:634-640`).
6. **False "escaped" on a momentary bob.** steve settles 300 ms and re-checks before declaring dry land, because "the bot pops to a dry Y for a single tick ... then the next step shoves it straight back in and it oscillates forever" (`steve/src/lib/steve/lib/bot-utils.ts:2991-3003`); `isOnDryLand` requires `onGround`, no water at feet or head, and non-water, non-lily support (`bot-utils.ts:2405-2417`). Two concurrent escape callers "alternated the control states every 350ms" (`bot-utils.ts:2584-2590`).
7. **Roofed aquifers: up is not always out.** rust-race-003 drowned in "a water column y 23–28 under solid stone at y 29–32 ... Breathable air was one block west and one down ... Every escape move went UP" (`CHANGES.md:283-290`). Underwater digging is ÷5 for eyes-in-water and ÷5 airborne; without the penalties "the capped-cave escape 'dug' its cap forever and drowned" (`CHANGES.md:262`).
8. **Lids.** A lily pad over the head stops the rise (`bot-utils.ts:2663-2676`, `CHANGES.md:1285-1291`).
9. **The aquifer drown-trap at the scoop.** steve's water search handed the bot cave water 23 blocks down; it dove in and was "HARD WATER-TRAPPED in the 1-wide flooded pocket", losing the run (memory `race-to-nether-walls.md`, race81/192). The fix was tiered: only source water with air above and `y >= botY-5`. Separately, two of the two deepest steve runs (498, 518) hung for 15-20 min at fill-water in dry biomes (`steve-water-find-wall.md`). `digExposesWater` refuses to break the last block holding water back (`bot-utils.ts:3341-3357`).
10. **Resumed tasks walk back in.** "the task it resumes (gather_wood or mine_stone in a treeless arena) re-enters the pool" (`CHANGES.md:310`): the escape must leave a fact behind that later planning consults.
11. **Lava.** ruststeve's `lava_unsafe_here` vetoes a cell when the floor is lava or non-solid with lava within 4 below, when any of the 0.6-wide body's columns has lava at floor/feet/head, or the 3×3 ring does (`ruststeve/src/tasks/lava_move.rs:10-19`); mining "NEVER dig[s] obsidian" (`ruststeve/src/tasks/mining.rs:115-118`). Deaths at the portal were pours onto the bot's own feet and tp cells over the sea (memory `ruststeve-nether-loop-2026-09.md`).

## Design

**Physics (`physics.clj`), pure and version-exact.** Add a `liquid?` oracle alongside `solid?`: `(fluid-at x y z) → nil | {:fluid/kind :water|:lava :fluid/level 0..15}` derived from `block-at` with ids 86-101 water and 102-117 lava (`resources/clojurecraft/blocks.edn:38-39`, level = id − base). Waterlogged states (`:waterlogged_transparent` etc.) count as full water. Liquid height per typecraft `getRenderedDepth`: level ≥ 8 → full, else `cy + 1 - (level+1)/9` (`physics.ts:660-670`). `in-water?` tests the AABB contracted by `(0.001 0.401 0.001)`, `in-lava?` by `(0.1 0.4 0.1)` (`physics.ts:1001-1005`). `eye-in-water?` tests the single block at `y + 1.62`, never feet+1 (trap 4). The liquid branch of `step`, as constants with a comment each: `liquid-acceleration 0.02`, `water-inertia 0.8`, `lava-inertia 0.5`, `water-gravity 0.005` (gravity/16 for 1.13+, `physics.ts:189`), `lava-gravity 0.02`, `swim-up 0.04` per tick while `:control/jump?` and in liquid (`physics.ts:1014-1016`), `swim-down 0.04` while `:control/sneak?` (`physics.rs:1101-1104`), `out-of-liquid-impulse 0.3` when `:player/horizontal-collision?` and the box moved by `[vx (+ vy 0.6) vz]` is free of solids and water. Order is vanilla's: heading, move, `vy *= inertia`, `vy -= gravity`, then the impulse. Fall distance: accumulate `:player/fall-distance` when `vy < 0` out of water, reset to 0 on landing or on entering water; vanilla damage is `(- fall 3)` so three blocks is free. New attributes, written by `step` every tick, never by anyone else: `:player/in-water?`, `:player/in-lava?`, `:player/eye-in-water?`, `:player/fall-distance`. `:player/air` (0-300) is tracked by the reducer from `eye-in-water?`: −1 per tick submerged, +4 per tick out, clamped; it is an estimate (the server does not send air for the own player except via entity metadata), and the escape goal keys off time submerged, not health.

**Blocks and memory.** Add `blocks/kind` → `:log | :water | :lava | nil` over the table so `memory/watched?` (`memory.clj:13`) becomes `(contains? #{:log :water :lava} (blocks/kind id))`; sightings keep `:block/state`, so a water sighting carries its level for free. Add `memory/nearest-surface-water` (source, air above, within 5 below the eye: trap 9) and `memory/lava-near?`. Water-trap facts: when `:escape-water` succeeds, record `{:trap/pos [x z] :trap/seen-at now :trap/kind :water}` under `:world/traps` (a new attribute, accreted, never a mutation of sightings); `:walk` and the pathfinder treat trap columns as high-cost for the next N minutes (trap 10).

**Goals and preemption.** `plan/goals` gains `{:goal/id :escape-water :goal/priority 0 :goal/preempt? true}` and `{:goal/id :escape-lava :goal/priority 0 :goal/preempt? true}`. `goal-done? :escape-water` is steve's `isOnDryLand` as a pure fn: `on-ground?`, not `in-water?`, no water at feet or eye, support block solid and not a lily pad, held for 6 consecutive ticks (trap 6, as a `:plan/dry-ticks` counter rather than a sleep). `next-intent :escape-water` returns `{:intent/kind :swim-out :intent/bank [x y z]}` where the bank is chosen once from memory and chunks: nearest column with air at eye height and solid support at or one below water level, preferring air columns below the feet when the eye column is roofed (trap 7); the executor holds `:control/forward? true :control/jump? true` toward that bank and keeps it (trap 2 and 3); after `stuck-ticks` with no `:player/pos` progress it escalates to `{:intent/kind :dig :intent/target cap}` straight up, or sideways toward the air column, with the dig deadline multiplied ×5 for eye-in-water and ×5 again when not on ground (trap 7, `intent.clj:17-18` is a constant today and must become a function of the world). A lily pad directly above is dug first (trap 8). `:escape-lava` is the same shape with `:intent/kind :flee` to the nearest non-lava footing. Preemption is one rule in `plan-tick`: when a `:goal/preempt?` goal is not done and `:plan/goal` differs, the active intent is moved to `:plan/abandoned` with `:intent/abandoned-at` and `:intent/reason :preempted`, `:player/controls` is cleared, `:plan/last` is dissoced so the new goal's `next-intent` never sees a stale `:intent/kind` (`wood.clj:24-26` dispatches on it), and the new intent starts in the same tick. Abandoned intents are not resumed; the goal re-derives them, which is already the design (`plan.clj:6-8`). Add `:plan/goal` beside `:plan/intent` so every intent knows which goal issued it; `:plan/intent` keeps its meaning.

**Pathfinder.** Cell costs, for the pathfinder issue to consume: water cell ×4, water deeper than one below its surface forbidden, any cell adjacent to lava forbidden, descents > 3 forbidden unless the landing is water (fall rule above), trap columns ×10. `:dig` refuses a target whose six neighbours include water or lava (steve's `digExposesWater`, trap 9) and refuses obsidian (trap 11).

**Respawn.** Add specs for s2c `:respawn` (82), `:player-combat-kill` (68), `:damage-event` (25) and c2s `:client-command` (12) to `packet/specs` (`packets.edn` already has the ids). On `set-health` with `health <= 0`: assoc `:player/dead? true`, inc `:stats/deaths`, dissoc `:plan/intent :plan/last`, clear controls, emit `{:packet/name :client-command :action 0}`. On `:respawn`: dissoc `:player/dead?`, reset `:world/entities`, `:player/vel`, `:player/fall-distance`, `:player/air`; the following `player-position` teleport is already handled (`game.clj:116-131`). `:plan/status` stays `:active`, so the next tick re-derives the goal from inventory (keep_inventory is on).

**Sim.** `test/clojurecraft/world.clj` `column` already takes arbitrary ids, so a pond is `{[x 63 z] 86 ...}`. `sim.clj` gains `:sim/air`, decrements it while the reported player position puts the eye in water, sends `set-health` −2 every 20 ticks at 0, and on `client-command 0` after health 0 sends `respawn` then a `player-position` to spawn.

**Specs.** `:player/in-water?` `:player/in-lava?` `:player/eye-in-water?` boolean; `:player/air` `(s/int-in 0 301)`; `:player/fall-distance` double; `:world/traps`; `:goal/preempt?`; `:plan/goal`; `:plan/abandoned`; `:control/sneak?`; the four packets.

## Steps

1. `blocks/kind`, `physics/fluid-at`, `in-water?`, `eye-in-water?`; unit tests on ids 86, 93, 101, 102 and a waterlogged state. Verify: `clojure -M:test`.
2. Liquid branch of `physics/step` with the constants above; `:player/fall-distance`. Verify: the numeric tests in Acceptance.
3. `memory` watches water and lava; `nearest-surface-water`; `:world/traps`. Verify: a column with a pond yields water sightings on load.
4. `:player/air`, `:player/dead?`, respawn packets and handlers; sim drowning and respawn. Verify: sim test where a bot is held under for 400 ticks dies and respawns at spawn with an empty intent.
5. Preemption in `plan-tick`, `:plan/goal`, `:plan/abandoned`. Verify: property test below.
6. `:swim-out` and `:flee` executors, `:escape-water` and `:escape-lava` goals, dig penalties as a function of the world. Verify: pond sim test.
7. Pathfinder costs and `:dig` refusals (hands off to the pathfinder issue).
8. Harness: `land!` variant `shore` that locates a river or beach biome and teleports to the water edge; CI matrix `wood` plus `shore`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit: from rest in a 2-deep pond, no controls, after 40 ticks `vy` converges to about −0.005/0.2 equilibrium and the bot sinks; with `:control/jump?` the feet rise to within 0.05 of the vanilla surface equilibrium (feet ≈ surface − 1.03, `CHANGES.md:515`) and `:player/eye-in-water?` is false there; with forward into a 1-high bank the impulse fires and the bot stands on the bank within 60 ticks; a 4-block fall sets `:player/fall-distance` 4.0 and water resets it; horizontal speed in water settles at 0.02/(1−0.8) = 0.1 blocks/tick.
- Sim: bot spawned (teleported) into a 2-deep pond with a bank three blocks away reaches `goal-done? :escape-water` within 10 s of sim time and never has `:player/air` below 200; a roofed 5×5×6 pool whose only air is a side pocket (ruststeve's `water_roofed`) is escaped within 90 s with zero deaths.
- Property (`props_test.clj`): for any generated world with an active `:walk` or `:dig` intent, setting `:player/in-water?` true and ticking once yields `:plan/intent` of kind `:swim-out`, `:plan/abandoned` holding the old intent, and `:player/controls` not containing the old look.
- Property: `step` never throws for a `set-health` of 0 followed by any sequence of packets, and `:plan/intent` is absent after it.
- Replay: a recorded pond run replays to the same `RESULT`.
- Live: the harness lands next to water (`shore` landing), the bot walks in on purpose via a test intent, and `RESULT` reports `:escaped true` with the RCON judge `execute as <name> at @s unless block ~ ~ ~ minecraft:water unless block ~ ~1 ~ minecraft:water` passing and `OnGround 1b` (steve's `onDryLand`, `steve/src/lib/steve/gym/registry.ts:130-137`).
- CI: a `shore` job in `.github/workflows/wood.yml` next to `wood`, modelled on steve's landing set W ("swimmable water 2+ deep, a bank 3-5 blocks away at most one block above the water", `registry.ts:124-126`), 10 trials, pass ≥ 9/10, zero deaths counted at the `set-health 0` edge.
