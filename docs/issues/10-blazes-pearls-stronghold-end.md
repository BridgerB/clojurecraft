# Roadmap: blaze rods, ender pearls, eyes of ender, the stronghold, the End and the dragon

## Summary

Everything after `enter-nether` is the frontier none of the three bots has crossed naturally. This issue is the roadmap for clojurecraft's share of it: give the world value a mob model (today `:world/entities` holds item drops only), add the handful of packets combat and projectiles need (the 775 `attack` packet first), write a `:fight` intent and a general `:collect-drops`, then stack the goals in the order the game forces: `blaze-rods` (>= 7) → `ender-pearls` (>= 12) → `eyes-of-ender` (12) → `stronghold` (eye throws + triangulation as a pure function) → `end-portal` (fill 12 frames) → `enter-end` → `dragon` (crystals, then the perched dragon) → win detection. Each sub-goal is a gym row that can be set up over RCON in isolation, so the roadmap does not wait on the Nether/fortress issue to start producing proof. The sim model gets mobs that die after N hits and an eye throw that reports a direction, so the planner path is exercised with no Java process.

## Why now / where the siblings are

The siblings have paid for most of the research; clojurecraft should inherit the facts, not the scars.

**The 775 attack packet.** ruststeve spent a whole session dealing zero damage because 26.1.2 split attacking out of `interact`. `src/bot/mod.rs:1607-1627` (`/Users/bridger/Developer/mc/upstream/ruststeve`): "775 (26.1.2) split attacking into a dedicated `attack` packet (0x01) — just the target entity id. The old `interact` packet (0x1a) is now right-click ONLY, so sending an interact-with-mouse=1 was a no-op right-click on the mob (decoded fine, dealt no damage; and without a `hand` it even failed to decode → kicked us)." typecraft agrees at `src/lib/typecraft/bot/entities.ts:840-841`: "26.x has a dedicated attack packet; interact with mouse=1 gets the bot kicked. `bot.client.write("attack", { target: target.id })`", and its protocol test pins the id: `expect(codec.packetIds.get("attack")).toBe(1)` (`protocol/protocol.test.ts:556`). Our generated table already has it: `resources/clojurecraft/packets.edn` lists `:attack 1` and `:interact 26` under play c2s; we only lack the spec. The same `attack` also notes the pose must be synced first: "Sync our current position+look to the server BEFORE attacking ... otherwise the server evaluates the attack's reach/angle against a STALE pose and rejects it" (`mod.rs:1613-1618`). In our reducer that is free: `movement-packets` (`game.clj:244-257`) emits `move-player-pos-rot` on the same tick as any look change, so the intent must set `:control/look` one tick before it emits `:attack`.

**Pacing.** ruststeve's second bug: `wait_ticks` collapsed under the packet flood and 40 swings fired in 66 ms, all but one inside the mob's 10-tick hurt-invulnerability. Fix: `wait_real_ms(650)` (`mod.rs:502-516`; `tasks/nether.rs:424`: "650ms of REAL time between swings: clears BOTH the mob's 10-tick (0.5s) hurt-invulnerability AND the iron sword's ~12.5-tick full-charge cooldown"). steve copied the number (`tasks/nether/main.ts:178-182`, `await sleep(650)`). For us the clock is an input on the tick event, so the cooldown is a deadline in the intent (`:intent/next-swing`, exactly like `:dig`'s `swing-every` at `intent.clj:19,136`), and the property "no two `:attack` effects within 600 ms of :time/now" is checkable in `props_test.clj`.

**What the blaze fight learned.** `tasks/nether.rs:330-520` (`kill_blaze`): defend against anything hostile within 4 blocks first ("3 of 10 deaths to wither skeletons the hunt never fought"); break off below 10 hp mid-fight ("the blaze died and so did the bot (fireball impacts; fire resistance only stops the burn)"); aim at `y + 0.9` because a blaze hovers; count only in-reach swings and blacklist an entity that survives 25 of them as a ghost; kill = the entity disappears from the map; then walk the drop. Memory note `ruststeve-post-nether-blaze.md`: "COMBAT SOLVED ... reproduced twice in the overworld ARENA_TEST arena", with the arena recipe in `arena-run.sh` (sealed netherrack box at 500,100,500, a `minecraft:spawner{SpawnData:{entity:{id:"minecraft:blaze"}}}`, a kitted bot with `Invulnerable:1b`, `summon minecraft:blaze` every cycle).

**Pearls.** ruststeve barters: `tasks/nether.rs:523-567` `barter_pearls` ("toss ONE gold ingot, wait for the 6 s barter, then walk over every item within 8 blocks ... Piglins attack a player without gold armour, so the kit wears a golden helmet"), gym `GymSetup::NetherPiglins` summons three piglins with `IsImmuneToZombification:1b` (`gym.rs:51-52,1174-1177`). steve hunts endermen (`tasks/combat/main.ts` `huntEndermen`, "Don't look at enderman's eyes before attacking"); its End notes record the cost: "f7 dragon-1/3/5 were all 'slain by Enderman' before a single bed" (`tasks/end/bed.ts:34-36`).

**Stronghold and End.** steve's `tasks/stronghold/find.ts` is a stub ("TODO: Track the eye entity, calculate direction, triangulate"); `activate.ts` fills frames by `placeBlock` on each `end_portal_frame` within 16 and checks for an `end_portal` block. Nobody has thrown an eye in anger yet; this is the one piece of the chain with no sibling proof. For the dragon both siblings went beds-not-bow: ruststeve `tasks/end.rs` `dragon_beds` ("pillar up east of the fountain ... on a real perch (low over the pillar, hd <= 3), place a bed on the bridge end and use it — beds explode in the End") and `crystals_bow`; steve `tasks/end/bed.ts`. The gyms summon what they need: `gym.rs:1046-1090` kills crystals, summons a dragon if none, sets `Health:200f` and `DragonPhase:0` ("A /summon'ed dragon ... has no fight manager and starts in phase 10 (hover)"); steve `gym/registry.ts:260-330` does the same and judges by RCON `execute in minecraft:the_end if entity @e[type=minecraft:ender_dragon]`. Memory `steve-cycle5-end-2026-10-09.md`: "RCON shows the server has NO beds on the bot in the End (client thinks 6) — kit loss on the cross-dim tp" — give the kit after the dimension change, never before.

**Peaceful.** Both race servers run Peaceful for long runs (memory: "Server B Peaceful"); blazes, endermen, piglins and the dragon's fight do not exist there. The gyms work around it per trial: `arena-run.sh` sends `difficulty normal` before building the arena; local servers pin `difficulty=normal` (`steve/local-server.sh:47`, `scripts/ci/server.sh:31`). Our `local-server.sh` and CI server must do the same for these rows, and the harness must summon the mobs rather than wait for spawns.

## Design

**Entity model.** `:world/entities` already exists, specced at `spec.clj:49-51` as `{eid {:entity/type int :entity/pos vec3}}`, and `game.clj:221-224` keeps only `blocks/item-entity-type`. Lift the filter: keep every `add-entity` (type ids from `entity-types.edn`: `:blaze 14`, `:enderman 41`, `:ender_dragon 43`, `:end_crystal 45`, `:eye_of_ender 50`, `:piglin 101`, `:small_fireball 118`, `:wither_skeleton 146`, `:item 71`). Add attributes beside the old ones, never changing them: `:entity/vel` (from `add-entity`'s velocity triple and `set-entity-motion`, i16/8000), `:entity/health` (from `set-entity-data`), `:entity/seen-at` (already written). Health is a metadata skim: read entries `[key u8][type varint][value]` until key 255, decode only `float` (type 3) at the living-entity health index and `byte` at index 0 (flags: on-fire bit for blazes); on any other type stop reading and keep what was decoded (the frame tail is ignorable exactly as `packet.clj:8-9` already does for chunks). The ordering gotcha (steve CLAUDE.md:76, "one off entry shifts every later type → stream desync") does not bite a skimmer that stops at the first type it does not model, but the table at `typecraft/protocol/generated-mappings.json:445-486` (0 byte, 1 int, 2 long, 3 float ... 39 humanoid_arm) is the reference if we ever decode more. `remove-entities` is the death signal for a mob (ruststeve and steve both use "entity gone"); `entity-event` status 3 on the id is the earlier one.

**Packet specs to add** (`packet/specs`, ids already generated): c2s `[:play :c2s :attack] [[:entity-id :varint]]`, `[:play :c2s :interact] [[:entity-id :varint] [:action :varint] [:hand :varint] [:sneaking :bool]]` (action 0 interact needs the hand — the decode kick above), `[:play :c2s :use-item] [[:hand :varint] [:sequence :varint] [:yaw :f32] [:pitch :f32]]`, `[:play :c2s :client-command] [[:action :varint]]`; s2c `:set-entity-motion [[:entity-id :varint] [:vx :i16] [:vy :i16] [:vz :i16]]`, `:teleport-entity [[:entity-id :varint] [:x :f64] [:y :f64] [:z :f64] ...]`, `:entity-event [[:entity-id :i32] [:status :i8]]`, `:damage-event [[:entity-id :varint] [:source-type :varint] [:source-cause :varint] [:source-direct :varint]]`, `:player-combat-kill [[:player-id :varint]]` (message is NBT, skip), `:set-entity-data [[:entity-id :varint] [:metadata :metadata]]` with a new `:metadata` reader, `:game-event [[:reason :u8] [:value :f32]]`, `:boss-event` (action 0 add / 1 remove / 2 health — the dragon's bar), and widen `:login`/`:respawn` to read the dimension name into `:world/dimension` (ruststeve `mod.rs:632-665` shows what else a dimension change must reset: chunks, entities, min-y/height 0/256 for nether and end). Field shapes are from `typecraft/protocol/packet-defs.ts:167-180,449-458,521-526,568-585,1067-1072,1416-1434,1859-1870,2786-2792,2405`.

**`:fight` intent** (`intent/run :fight`, new namespace `combat.clj`): stages `:approach` (reuse `toward`, stop at 3.0 horizontal), `:engage` (set `:control/look` at target `y+0.9`; when `:time/now >= :intent/next-swing` and distance <= 3.2 emit `set-carried-item` (sword slot), `swing`, `attack`; `next-swing = now + 650`), `:retreat` when `:player/health < 10` (walk 6 blocks away along the bot→mob vector, then `done`/`failed :hurt`), fail `:ghost` after 25 in-reach swings, done when the id leaves `:world/entities`. Fireball dodging is deferred: ruststeve never needed it with fire resistance off and 10-hp break-off; revisit with the damage-event log. The planner's `fail-intent` blacklist already takes `:intent/target`; make the target an eid for `:fight` and widen `:intent/target` spec to `(or ::block-pos int?)`.

**`:collect-drops`**: generalise `:collect` (`intent.clj:148-168`), which is done only when `logs-held` is positive, to `{:intent/kind :collect-drops :intent/item id :intent/count n}` done when the inventory count rises or no item entity remains within 8 blocks.

**Goals** (rows in `plan/goals`, priorities after `enter-nether`):
- `:blaze-rods` done when `blaze_rod >= 7` (item 1117); next-intent: defend (nearest hostile within 4) → `:fight` the nearest blaze within 12 → `:collect-drops` → else walk to the remembered spawner (`memory/watched?` gains `spawner` 3888 and `nether_bricks`) → `{:plan/wait :no-blaze}`.
- `:ender-pearls` done when `ender_pearl >= 12` (item 1116). Decision: **barter, not hunt.** Reasons: piglins come to the gold, endermen must be found and provoked; bartering is ranged and never needs the look-away rule that killed steve's runs; a stack of gold is a dig we already know how to do (nether gold ore is everywhere in the Nether; ruststeve's gym just gives it); hunting needs ~30 kills for 12 pearls at the 50% drop. Intents: `:toss` (new: `container-click` throw-out of one `gold_ingot` 909 while looking at the piglin), wait 6 s, `:collect-drops ender_pearl`. Kit: golden helmet worn, otherwise `:fight` is forced.
- `:eyes-of-ender` done when 12 eyes held: `blaze_powder` 1125 from rods (2×2 craft) then eye = powder + pearl (2×2, no table). Depends on the crafting work in the earlier issues.
- `:stronghold`: `:throw-eye` intent — `set-carried-item` eye, `use-item`, then watch for the next `add-entity` with type 50 within 2 blocks: record `[p0 t0]`, then the position at `t0 + 2000` ms (eye flight ~ 3 s, driven by `set-entity-motion`/`teleport-entity`/`move-entity-pos`), yielding `{:throw/from p0 :throw/dir unit-xz}` appended to `:stronghold/throws`. `triangulate` is a pure function over two throws: intersection of the two 2-D rays (`from + t·dir`), returning `[x z]` or nil when parallel; the goal walks ~300 blocks along the first direction before throwing again and declares the stronghold found when the intersection is within 100 of the bot and `end_portal_frame` (9469) sightings exist, else digs down to y 20 at the intersection. All of that is a `defmethod` in `stronghold.clj`.
- `:end-portal` done when an `end_portal` (9468) block is remembered: for each of the 12 remembered frames whose state has `eye=false` (block-state table), `:walk` then `use-item-on` face 1 with the eye selected.
- `:enter-end` done when `:world/dimension` = `minecraft:the_end`: walk into the portal block; on `respawn` reset chunks/entities (new reducer branch).
- `:dragon`: `:crystals` sub-goal (10 end_crystal entities; the siblings' bow work is 1800 s budgets and cages — first version pillars up each tower with blocks and hits the crystal with `attack`, as any damage pops it), then `:perch-fight`: stand at the fountain (0, ~63, 0), when the dragon entity sits within 6 horizontal and y < 70 for 10 ticks, `:fight` it at 650 ms; retreat on breath (`area_effect_cloud`/`dragon_fireball` within 4). Dragon health 200 (`gym.rs:1082` sets `Health:200f`), tracked via `:entity/health` or the boss bar.
- Win: `:world/dragon-dead?` set by `entity-event` status 3 on the dragon's eid, or `remove-entities` of it with the boss bar removed, or `update-advancements` carrying `minecraft:end/kill_dragon`; `game-event` reason 4 (`win_game`) is the credits signal when the bot walks into the exit portal, answered with `client-command 0` (typecraft `bot/game.ts:186-192`). RESULT `:ok` on `dragon-dead?`; the CI judge is RCON `execute in minecraft:the_end if entity @e[type=minecraft:ender_dragon]` returning failed.

**Per-difficulty strategy.** The goal table does not know the difficulty; the harness does. Gym rows set `difficulty normal`, forceload, summon (`summon minecraft:blaze {NoAI:1b}` for the first combat row, piglins with `IsImmuneToZombification`, a dragon with `DragonPhase:0`), and give the kit after confirming `execute as <bot> at @s if dimension ...`. A race on Peaceful cannot pass these goals; say so in the RESULT (`:blocked :peaceful`) rather than wait.

**Sim extensions** (`sim.clj`): `:sim/mobs {eid {:pos :health :hits}}` spawned by a fixture, an `:attack` handler that decrements health when the player is within 3.2 and >= 500 ms since the last hit, emits `remove-entities` and an item at death; a `:use-item` handler that, when the held item is an eye, emits `add-entity` type 50 and three `teleport-entity`s along a fixed direction toward `:sim/stronghold`; a `:respawn` on walking into `:sim/portal`. Specs: `:entity/health double?`, `:entity/vel ::vec3`, `:world/dimension string?`, `:stronghold/throws`, `:intent/kind` additions, and `s/fdef` for `triangulate`.

## Steps (ordered checkpoints, each a gym row)

1. `entities`: every `add-entity` kept with `:entity/vel`; `set-entity-data` health skim; property: step total over generated metadata. Judge: a summoned pig's health in the atom equals RCON `data get entity`.
2. `attack`: arena (ruststeve's box recipe), one `{NoAI:1b}` zombie, `:fight` kills it; judge: entity gone + 650 ms spacing in the record file.
3. `blaze-1`: arena with a blaze spawner, kitted invulnerable bot, 1 rod. 4. `blaze-7`: same, 7 rods, not invulnerable, break-off exercised.
5. `pearls`: crimson forest landing, 3 piglins, 12 gold given, 12 pearls.
6. `eyes`: rods + pearls given, 12 eyes crafted.
7. `throw`: one eye, the recorded run shows a direction within 10° of RCON `locate structure stronghold`.
8. `stronghold`: two throws, triangulate, walk/dig, frames remembered.
9. `portal`: tp into a located portal room, 12 eyes, `end_portal` remembered.
10. `enter-end`: `:world/dimension` flips, chunks reset, bot standing in the End (judge: RCON dimension).
11. `crystals`: 10 crystals summoned, all gone. 12. `dragon`: crystals cleared, dragon summoned, dead; RESULT `:ok true`.
13. `chain`: steps 3→12 back to back on one server from a given Nether kit, then the natural run once the Nether issue lands.

## Acceptance criteria

- `packet/specs` has the ten packets above; `props_test` stays total over them; `attack` encodes to `[0x01 varint]`.
- `:world/entities` tracks mobs with health; nothing in `game.clj` branches on entity type except the metadata skim.
- `:fight` is a defmethod with no clock reads; the record of a fight shows >= 600 ms between attacks and a retreat below 10 hp.
- `triangulate` is pure and unit-tested on crossing, parallel and behind-the-bot rays.
- `sim_test` runs `:blaze-rods` (mob fixture) and `:stronghold` (eye fixture) to `plan/done?` with no server.
- Gym rows 1-12 pass on the local 25571 server at `difficulty normal`; row 12's RESULT `:ok true` is confirmed by the RCON dragon query.
- No Peaceful race waits on a mob: `:blocked :peaceful` in the summary.

## Open questions (what we cannot know until the Nether/fortress issue lands)

- Where the bot stands when `enter-nether` completes (portal pocket, lava sea edge, fortress distance) decides whether `:blaze-rods` starts with fortress-finding or spawner-camping; ruststeve's `NetherPortalPocket` gym exists because its arrival was boxed in by its own frame.
- Whether the return trip reuses the same portal (needs `:memory` of the overworld portal position across a dimension change) or casts a new one with leftover buckets.
- Kit at Nether entry: is there iron for a sword and gold for bartering, or does the Nether leg have to mine nether gold ore first? That sets the priority between `:blaze-rods` and `:ender-pearls`.
- Chunk/height reset on `respawn`: the Nether issue will decide whether `:world/chunks` is keyed by dimension or cleared; the End reuses whatever it picks.
- Whether the bot has a crafting table in the Nether (eyes need none, blaze powder needs none, a sword does).
