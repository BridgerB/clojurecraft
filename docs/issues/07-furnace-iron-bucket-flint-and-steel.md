# Furnace, coal, iron, buckets, flint and steel: the iron kit that precedes the portal

## Summary

Add the goals and intents that take the bot from a stone pickaxe to the portal kit: a placed furnace, fuel, raw iron, iron ingots, one or two buckets, flint, and a flint and steel. This is the stretch of the chain where both siblings spent most of their race time and where no steve race ever got through cleanly. The work is: a `:smelt` intent that is a pure plan over furnace-window state; a `:use-item-on` intent (bucket on water) confirmed by inventory, never by the packet echo; ores, gravel, water and lava as watched block kinds in `memory` with a `nearest` query by kind; fuel as a consumable need in the goal table so the planner can never arrive at the furnace with nothing to burn; and a sim furnace with real timing so all of it runs in `sim_test` before it touches a server. Depends on the crafting (2x2/3x3), block-placing, pathfinder and dig-with-tools issues.

## Why now / what the siblings learned

Three walls capped every steve race on this stretch. They are documented, not folklore.

**Fuel deadlock.** `race-to-nether-walls.md`: on the fastest cell of the session the bot "SMELTED cleanly ... to 3 iron_ingot ... then STALLED. Root cause: the smelt burns planks as furnace fuel, which runs dry after ~3 ingots, and the bots don't mine coal for fuel ... No bot passed 3 ingots". Fuel was never a planner need; it was an emergent side effect of whatever wood happened to be in the bag. The fixes are a thicket of counters in `steps.ts`: `needsSmeltFuel` (lines 23-28, `coal < 1 && planks < 4 && logs < 1`), the `gather_wood.isComplete` fuel sub-clause (lines 116-139, `coal >= 1 || planks >= 8 || logs >= 2`), `mine_coal.isComplete` with five escape hatches (lines 276-296), `smelt_iron.canExecute` (lines 344-348). `smelt/main.ts:175-184` finally orders coal before wood ("the fuel-at-smelt deadlock that caps races at ~3 ingots"). ruststeve hit the inverse: `LOOP.md:90` "Coal over-target — stalled 30 min mining a 3rd coal it never needed".

**Undercount.** `steve-wood-lock-diagnosis.md`: `countItem` "reads only the inventory window ... During a craft the log stack sits in the crafting GRID (invisible ...)", so a higher-priority step preempts the craft every tick. The same bug reappears at the furnace: `smelt/main.ts:70-73` "windowItems can't see grid slots, which causes a phantom 'no furnace' hot-spin", and ruststeve's `smelt.rs:115-119` "While a furnace window is open the live inventory lives in the window — `bot.inventory` is stale until the window closes". Both bots have two inventories that disagree. `smelt/main.ts:199-229` then polls output with sleeps and a 90 s deadline chosen to be "BELOW the run-loop's ~120s per-step timeout" (line 208) because the step was killed mid-wait with ingots still in the furnace.

**Water-find.** `steve-water-find-wall.md`: serial 498 "did a full clean climb ... 2 buckets ... Then it hung at `Fill Water Buckets`" in a dry inland jungle, and 518 repeated it in a savanna: "two of the two deepest runs both died here". The other half is the aquifer: `race-to-nether-walls.md` race81/192 "FOUND water fast, but the water was UNDERGROUND cave water at y41 ... got HARD WATER-TRAPPED". `bucket/main.ts:102-137` `pickWater` now tiers sources (open-sky `air` above, near the column's terrain surface, else "return null so the caller escalates"), and lines 217-288 are a 70-line directional hunt. And steve cannot remember water at all: `bot-utils.ts:1303-1305` "NOT 'water': it's so common that the blockSeen firehose blocks the event loop".

**Right-click.** `typecraft/bot/inventory.ts:417-418` `activateItem` sets `usingHeldItem = true` and only `deactivateItem`/`consume` clear it; `bucket/main.ts:459-466` resets the flag by hand after every scoop. ruststeve `LOOP.md:37`: "the `use_item` pour/scoop is a POV raycast whose result echo lags; a scoop reports empty though the source was there", and `bucket.rs:77-93` writes a predicted `water_bucket` into the inventory when the echo is late. `CHANGES.md:133`: success is "filled-bucket count rose (delta vs entry), not `> 0`".

**What we do differently.** Fuel is a `:goal/needs` entry like any other item, with burn values as data, so the planner provides fuel before it chooses `:smelt` and the deadlock is a property test, not a counter. There is one inventory: `:player/inventory` plus `:window/*` attributes, both written by the reducer from the same packets, so nothing is ever invisible. Ores, gravel, water and lava are sightings with `:block/seen-at`, derived from chunk loads with no scanning and no firehose (they are a filter over `chunk/find-blocks` at load, as logs are today in `memory/remember-column`). Right-click success is a fact in the inventory, never a flag.

## Design

**Goal rows** (`plan/goals`, in priority order after the stone pickaxe; each `:goal/done?` is re-derived from the world every tick as `plan/choose` does now):

- `:furnace` needs `{:item/cobblestone 8 :block/crafting-table :near}`, done when a furnace is remembered within reach or held. Place through the placing issue's `:place` intent.
- `:fuel` needs nothing; done when `(fuel-ticks world) >= (* 200 (raw-iron-to-smelt world))`. `fuel-ticks` sums burn values from a data table `{:item/coal 1600 :item/charcoal 1600 :item/planks 300 :item/log 300 :item/stick 100}` over the inventory. Next intent: dig a remembered `coal_ore`/`deepslate_coal_ore` if one is within radius, else craft planks from spare logs (never below the wood reserve the crafting issue keeps), else the charcoal fallback: smelt logs with planks as fuel. 1 coal = 8 items, so 1 coal covers the kit; sticks are listed so leftovers count.
- `:raw-iron` needs a stone pickaxe; done at `raw_iron + iron_ingot + 3*buckets + flint_and_steel >= 4` (steve `steps.ts:315-319` counts invested iron the same way; without it the bot re-mines iron it already spent). Next intent: `:dig` the nearest remembered `iron_ore`/`deepslate_iron_ore`.
- `:iron-ingots` needs `{:block/furnace :near :item/raw-iron 1 :fuel 200}`; done at `iron_ingot + 3*buckets + flint_and_steel >= 4`. Next intent: `:smelt`.
- `:bucket` needs `{:item/iron-ingot 3 :block/crafting-table :near}`, done at one bucket (two when the portal issue asks). `:flint` needs a stone pickaxe, done at one flint; next intent digs the nearest remembered gravel, and re-places and re-digs the dropped gravel on the same spot until flint drops (10 percent per break, no limit on re-digs). `:flint-and-steel` needs `{:item/iron-ingot 1 :item/flint 1}`, shapeless, 2x2 is enough.

**The `:smelt` intent** is a pure plan over window state. New reducer attributes: `:window/id`, `:window/type`, `:window/slots` (vector of slot maps), `:window/state-id`, `:window/carried`, `:window/data {prop value}`. `game/on-packet` gains `[:play :open-screen]`, `[:play :container-set-data]`, `[:play :container-close]`, and `:container-set-content`/`:container-set-slot` for non-zero window ids (today `game.clj:213-219` drops them). Vanilla furnace layout: slot 0 input, 1 fuel, 2 output, 3-29 main inventory, 30-38 hotbar; `container-set-data` properties 0 fuel left, 1 fuel total, 2 cook progress, 3 cook total (200). Stages: `:open` emits `use-item-on` (c2s 66) at the furnace and waits for `open-screen`; `:load` emits `container-click` (c2s 18) pairs computed from `:window/slots` (pick fuel stack, put slot 1; pick raw iron, put slot 0) and waits until the slot echo matches; `:cook` waits on `:window/data` and `:window/slots 2`, with `:intent/finish-at` = now + 200 ticks * count * 50 ms + slack as the deadline, re-loading fuel when property 0 hits zero and input remains; `:take` clicks output to an empty inventory slot, then reclaims leftover fuel and input (steve `smelt/main.ts:252-264`); `:close` emits `container-close` (c2s 19) only with nothing carried. Done when `:player/inventory` shows the ingots. Every wait is an absolute `:time/now` comparison; there is no sleep.

**The `:use-item-on` intent** (fill bucket): `:settle` looks at the source block centre from a dry shore cell (the placing issue's stand finder) and selects the bucket slot with `set-carried-item`; `:use` emits `use-item-on` (66: hand, pos, face, cursor, inside-block, world-border-hit, sequence) and, if the server ignores it, `use-item` (67: hand, sequence, yaw, pitch); `:confirm` is done only when `:player/inventory` contains `water_bucket`, retrying after an absolute deadline up to N times, then failing with `:no-scoop` so the planner blacklists the block. No `usingHeldItem` equivalent exists because nothing is remembered that the inventory can tell us.

**Memory.** `memory/watched?` becomes a set of kinds: logs, `coal_ore`, `deepslate_coal_ore`, `iron_ore`, `deepslate_iron_ore`, `gravel`, `water` (source states only, level 0), `lava` (source). Add `(memory/nearest world kind eye radius blacklist)` generalising `nearest-log`, with the kind predicate in `blocks` (`ore?`, `gravel?`, `water-source?`, `lava-source?`). Water is kept despite volume because sightings are a filter over the paletted section at load, not an event per block; if a column's water count is large, keep one sighting per 4x4 column of its top layer (surface water is all we want anyway).

**Water-find strategy.** Candidates come from sightings, not scans: source water whose remembered block above is air and whose y is within 3 of the column's terrain surface (heightmap from the chunk packet, which we already decode as `:heightmaps`). Cave water is never a candidate. When no candidate is within radius, the planner emits a `:walk` toward the lowest terrain among eight bearings and records `[:region/dry xz-cell seen-at]` so the hunt does not revisit; the bot surfaces before hunting (dig-up intent from the pathfinder issue) because every remembered pond is above the mine.

**Sim extension.** `sim.clj` gains: a furnace block at a position; `open-screen` on `use-item-on` against it; a window with slots 0-2 plus the player inventory mirror; `container-click` with vanilla pick/place semantics and state ids; cooking at 200 ticks per item with fuel consumed per the burn table and `container-set-data` emitted each tick; ore blocks that drop `raw_iron`/`coal`; gravel that drops flint with `:event/rand` under 0.1 (randomness from the tick, so replay is exact); a water source that turns a held `bucket` into `water_bucket` on `use-item-on`.

**Specs.** `:window/*` attributes, `:item/*` need keys, `:goal/needs` as a map of need to count or `:near`, `:intent/kind` additions, `:effect/kind` unchanged. Burn table and furnace layout are `def`s with one-line comments, not literals in code.

## Steps

1. Reducer: window packets (`open-screen`, `container-set-data`, `container-close`, non-zero `container-set-content`/`-slot`) and the `:window/*` attributes; `container-click`/`container-close`/`use-item`/`use-item-on` c2s specs. Verify: `packet_test` round-trips each spec; a `props_test` property that `step` stays total over generated window packets.
2. Memory: watched kinds, `nearest`, water-source filter, heightmap surface. Verify: unit tests on a hand-built column with ore, gravel, a surface pond and a cave pool; the cave pool is never returned.
3. Burn table, `fuel-ticks`, `:fuel` goal. Verify: unit tests on inventories; the property in acceptance.
4. `:smelt` intent against the sim furnace. Verify: `sim_test` smelts 4 raw iron with 1 coal and the ingots land in `:player/inventory`; a second test with 3 planks runs out of fuel after 1 ingot and reloads.
5. `:use-item-on` against the sim water. Verify: `sim_test` fills a bucket; a test where the server never answers fails with `:no-scoop` after the retry budget.
6. Goal rows for furnace, raw iron, ingots, bucket, flint, flint and steel; flint re-place loop. Verify: `plan_test` picks each goal in order from a hand-built world.
7. Live: `clojure -M:run` on the local 25571 server from a landing with a RCON-given stone pickaxe; read inventory over RCON.
8. CI gym rows (see below).

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit: every new packet spec round-trips; `fuel-ticks`, `nearest`, surface filter, iron accounting (`invested iron` counts buckets and flint and steel) each have example tests.
- Property: for any generated inventory and raw-iron count, `plan/choose` never returns `:iron-ingots` unless `(fuel-ticks world) >= 200 * min(raw-iron, 8)`; for any generated sequence of window packets the `:smelt` plan never emits `container-close` while `:window/carried` is non-nil, and never emits a click on a slot index outside the window.
- Sim end to end: from a stone pickaxe, cobblestone, logs and a column with coal, iron, gravel and a surface pond, the composed reducer reaches furnace placed, ingots >= 4, bucket filled, flint and steel held, under a fixed tick budget, and `clojure -M:replay` of the recording reproduces the final world exactly.
- Live: one run on the local server reaches a filled `water_bucket` and `flint_and_steel` with the RCON inventory read as judge.
- CI: one workflow row per sub-goal, each landing the bot with RCON `give` of the prerequisites (pickaxe and cobble for furnace; furnace and raw iron and coal for smelt; three ingots for bucket; bucket for fill-water; ingot for flint and steel), judged by the `RESULT` line plus RCON.

## Open questions

- `container-click` on 775 carries hashed slot stacks for the changed-slots array and the carried item; confirm the exact field layout from the generated reports before writing the spec, and whether sending empty arrays is accepted (the siblings' SDKs do).
- Does the server echo `container-set-slot` for every click, or only on mismatch with the client's `state-id`? The `:load` stage's wait condition depends on this; `--record` one manual furnace session first.
- Water sightings: filter to surface layer per 4x4 column, or keep everything and measure? Decide from the sighting count on the CI forest seed.
- Bucket count: one bucket here and let the portal issue raise it, or two now so the lava side is covered? Steve's `KIT_BUCKETS` ended at 3; ruststeve casts with one water bucket and refills lava in place.
- Charcoal fallback: is it worth a second furnace cycle, or do we always require coal and accept a longer mine on coal-poor cells?
