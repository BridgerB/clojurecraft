# Gather chain

Getting a log is a chain of three intents, each started by the planner after the previous one finished: walk to the trunk, dig the log, collect the drop. `wood.clj` owns it and any goal that needs logs reuses it (`:wood` directly, `:kit` through the recipe graph's `:gather`).

## Key files
- `wood.clj`, `return-walk` - the last resort before `:no-log` on open ground: search up to `return-max-nodes` expansions for a complete route to one of the nearest standing trunks. The walk carries `:intent/max-nodes`, used both by `intent/routable?` and by its executor's `route!`, including replans. Issue #186's R2/17 stood on an exposed mountain ledge near trees, but reaching one required hundreds of waypoints down the mine stairs and around the mountain. The local 6000-node searches were partial; the bounded return search reaches the tree, gathers the log, makes the replacement kit and resumes the cast.
- `wood.clj`, `gather-next` - continue the chain if one is in flight, else start toward the nearest remembered trunk bottom a route reaches (`near-walk`: `memory/nearest-log`, radius 48, no more than 12 above or below the eye, of the three nearest the first `intent/routable?` accepts) at the log nearest feet height (`trunk-target`); when even that log is `intent/pickup-rise` or more over the feet, a trunk on a bank no jump climbs, `log-walk` asks for the `:pickup` goal (`:intent/pickup?`: the walk ends where the drop lies in the pickup box), so a trunk reached only from the foot of the bank has no route and is passed over: the forest property at FOREST_SEED 388575908371 dug a trunk on a two-high mound from the ground three cells off, its drop rested on the mound top out of the box, the collect timed out and a second log was dug for the first (sim test `a-trunk-on-a-two-high-mound-is-not-dug-from-its-foot`). A trunk no route reaches is passed over, never walked at: sim race 38395033917 (world R3/24, 108 runs, issue #75) stood at the bottom of two staircases that met, the second having dug out the first's riser, with no way up; each walk to a trunk in the band failed `:no-path` the tick it started and blacklisted one trunk, twelve a second, until the plan failed. With none in that band a route reaches, and a trunk remembered further off, `climb-out`: walk to the nearest within 256 that `intent/routable?` accepts, under the open sky or not. Not out of the ground (`stairs/out?`: the open sky over the feet and a level step onto open ground beside them; a pit under the sky is not out), with none of those a route reaches, walk up the air already dug (`intent/opening`), and cut stairs only when that mouth cannot be walked to, `stairs/climb-to` a length at a time ([[stairs-down]]). Under the open sky with logs remembered and no route to any, `hunt`: a walk toward the trees the way a water hunt goes out (`scoop/hunt-targets`, the ground forty blocks out along eight bearings, never nearer the landing; [[scoop-intent]]), to the ground nearest the nearest remembered log that a route reaches, tagged `:intent/for :log-hunt`, so the chunks on the way are remembered and the next pass finds the way round. Race 38477665442 bots Clj_r14 and Clj_r20 landed on a lake's shore with the forest across the water, every trunk in the near band and no route to one, and waited `:no-log` until the plan failed (issue #57, `with-no-route-to-any-trunk-the-bot-walks-toward-the-trees`); bot Clj_r15 came up out of its mine for a spare pickaxe's wood with the trees a hundred blocks off, past the pathfinder's budget, and waited the same way (issue #87). With no log remembered at all within `far-radius` the hunt sets out all the same, its targets in the water hunt's order: race 38665691517 runner 1 (issue #57) landed slots Clj_r08, Clj_r14 and Clj_r15 where no standing trunk was in reach and routes led out on three bearings, and each waited `:no-log` at its landing round after round (`with-no-tree-known-the-wood-hunt-sets-out`). With logs remembered within `far-radius` the hunt may also walk a hop back toward the landing (`scoop/hunt-targets` with `homeward?`, ranked only by how near the trees it lands): race 38680745815 runner 5 (issue #57) bot Clj_r10 hunted a hop away from its landing before any trunk was remembered, and with the valley's trunks then known below it, every bearing back toward them was refused as nearer the landing, the one bearing left had no route, and the plan waited `:no-log` out its clock (`a-hunt-that-knows-its-trees-walks-back-toward-the-landing`). Race 38477665442 (issues #56, #58, #54): the stone mine dug the bot two deep beside a cliff, onto a ledge of its quarry, onto a lakeshore under a cliff; every trunk was up a wall no jump climbs, twelve walks failed `:no-path`, and the sky over the feet said there was nothing to cut (`a-pit-under-the-sky-is-climbed-out-of`). The same three rules cover a hole under an oak canopy, where every exit jump meets leaves (solid to a route) and the sky is still over the feet: race 38477665442 runner 10 Clj_r16 and the sim race in world R2/28 (issue #63, the sim bot below a snow-lined slope, #38's wall at the second log) failed twelve walks `:no-path` before them; `a-pocket-under-a-canopy-is-climbed-out-of` holds that stairs are cut, and sim test `a-log-is-gathered-from-a-pocket-under-a-canopy` that the bot gets out and takes the log. Nor is the floor of a quarry out, though a level step crosses it: race 38515209959 (issues #102 and #103, 30 real bots) and the sim race in worlds R3/36 and R3/32 stood at the bottom of the quarry the stone mine had dug round their feet, two or three deep and a few cells wide, the stairs they came down by eaten by the digs, with the trees seven blocks off and no route to any; `stairs/out?` said out for the sky and the step, the hunt had no route either, and each waited `:no-log` for the table's log. A pit is a floor with a wall every way (`stairs/pit-rim`: along each heading, within eight columns, one the feet cannot step into, and ground over the feet in at least one of them), and `out?` is never true in one (`a-quarry-with-a-level-step-across-its-floor-is-climbed-out-of`; sim test `a-log-is-gathered-out-of-a-quarry` cuts up out of it by hand and takes the log). Boxed under water (the feet in `:plan/boxed-at`, where a climb out was boxed) with water within `bed-reach` (4) over the head, `into-water`: the lowest solid cell over the head dug first, `:intent/exposes-ok?` (the dig lets the water in), never when a cell between would fall on the bot (gravel, sand), is lava, unloaded or never dug; then, nothing but air and water over the head, a `:swim-out` `:intent/for :rise` up to the water's top (`:intent/to-y`) and to the shore ([[water-escape]]), done on dry land in the open. Only water open at the top: the cell over the water's run is air or a lake's ice (`stairs/lake-ice`: the rise digs the ice cap, [[water-escape]], issue #71's frozen sea); water under rock is a pocket with no way up, and the dig lets it flood the bot's tunnel (sim world R3/13, issue #71: an aquifer seven deep under stone, `boxed-under-water-that-has-no-top-the-bot-does-not-dig-into-it`). Sim race 38806318231 runner 1 and 38811426215 runner 8 (issue #89, worlds R4/30, R4/21 and R1/32 at 76010d2, 146 runs) had bots mine under the sea floor until the last pickaxe broke; for a new one they needed a log, every climb out was `:boxed` under the water, no route led home, and each waited `:no-log` (`boxed-under-the-sea-the-bot-digs-up-into-it`). With no trunk remembered, `{:plan/wait :no-log}`. The `:gather` act (`make`'s `plan/act :gather`) is `gather-or-out`: when `gather-next` waits `:no-log`, the way out the water hunt takes first (`way-out`, never in water, which the escape owns: `scoop/pit-climb` out of a pit under the sky deep under the landing, or from a cell under rock where a climb out died `:boxed` a `stone/retreat` to new rock); `make/spare-worth?` still asks `gather-next` alone, so a spare never sends the bot out this way. Sim race 38843552659 (issue #186): world R1/18 stood on a ravine's floor at y 6, sixty under its landing, and world R4/39 under rock at y 47 where its climb out had died `:boxed`; both waited `:no-log` with trees remembered sixty blocks off (`a-pit-deep-under-the-landing-is-climbed-out-of-for-wood`).
- `wood.clj`, `climb-out` takes the remembered logs nearest first and only then asks which are standing trunks (`standing-trunk?`, a memory query a log), taking the first three: sim race 38811417241 runner 2 (issue #87, world R2/20) remembered 20,223 logs, asked all of them every planner tick, and spent about 2.2 s of wall time a tick while it waited.
- `wood.clj`, `crossing` - last before the wait: under the open sky with no walk, climb or hunt reaching a tree and the nearest remembered standing trunk across open water (`water-toward?`: a column within `cross-reach` 48 along the way whose top is water), a `:cross` toward it ([[water-escape]]: swum at the surface with jump held, done on dry land once wet and `cross-gain` 8 nearer). Sim race 38811417241 runner 2 (issue #87, world R3/4): the stone pickaxe broke, a new one wanted a log, and the bot stood on a sand island at its landing with every trunk a hundred blocks across a lake; it waited `:no-log` until the plan failed; a crossing that lands short, an inlet waded, is followed by the next (`on-an-island-the-bot-swims-for-the-trees`). A spare is never worth a crossing (`make/spare-worth?`).
- `wood.clj`, `continue-gather` - reads `:plan/last` (the intent that just finished); only intents tagged `:intent/for :log` continue. The plain links (walk → dig → collect) are `intent/chain-next`, the same chain a mining row's `continue-mine` follows ([[mine-act]]); wood adds the leaf clears and their resume.
- `wood.clj`, `max-clears` - at most 6 leaf digs per drop.

## How it works
| Last intent | Next |
|---|---|
| `:walk` (for :log) | `:dig` the same target |
| `:dig` (for :log) | its `:intent/resume` if it had one, else `:collect` the same target |
| `:collect` with `:intent/blocked-by` and fewer than 6 clears | `:dig` the blocking leaf, carrying `:intent/resume` = the same collect with `:intent/clears` + 1 |
| anything else | nil (start fresh) |

On a sheet of ice (`path/on-ice?`) `gather-next` takes `stone/off-ice` after the near walk and before any climb: the route search treats the sheet as floor for a bot standing on it ([[pathfinder]]), so the trees on the shore are walked to.

## Gotchas
- A boxed cell with no retreat is climbed out of, not waited in. `way-out` ends in `stone/climb` (stairs up for `:boxed`, as the mine's last resort) after `stone/retreat`: a real bot (issue #89, Clj_r32) smelted its iron at y 16 in a cell a stair down had died `:boxed` in, no trunk within the near band, the retreat a walk at the snapshot's moment and none by the time the wait ran out, and the plan waited `:no-log` out beside a stone pickaxe (`a-boxed-cell-with-no-retreat-is-climbed-out-of-for-wood`).
- A boxed climb under gravel must not wait just because its way back is shorter than `stone/retreat-distance`. The gather act's `way-out` uses `stone/retreat`, which accepts the last unboxed waypoint of an exhausted search from boxed feet when the full distance is unreachable. Issue #370 (R1/29) had eight reachable tunnel cells, none twelve blocks away; gathering now walks away and re-plans a climb while the gravel and boxed-cell guards remain intact.
- A mouth below the feet is the way down, not out. `climb-out` walks the opening (`intent/opening`) only when `opening-up` finds its mouth no more than `opening-drop` (2) under the feet (`mouth-below?`); else the staircase up is cut on from where the feet are. Sim world R2/16 (issue #87): stairs cut up from the mouth at y 83 toward the trees on a cliff ended on a ledge at y 99 that is not out of the ground (`pit-rim`), the opening walked the bot back down to the mouth, and the climb began again every twenty seconds until the plan waited `:no-log` (`a-mouth-below-the-feet-is-the-way-down-not-out`).
- A close trunk no route reaches is climbed toward. `trees-bridge`, the last of `climb-out`'s branches (after `trees-climb` and `trees-descend`, so a trunk above the eye keeps its climb): out of the ground, a standing trunk within `bridge-reach` (12) on the plane and `bridge-rise` (3) of the feet's height, the feet less than `bridge-rise` over its bottom, no open water on the way (`water-toward?`: crossing's), never from a cell where a climb died `:boxed`, gets `bridge-climb` (2) stairs up toward it, `:intent/to-height?`; the steps are laid from carried filler where the air has none, and the next look from higher finds the way down onto the far side. At most two such climbs per trunk height. Sim worlds R3/45 (issues #87 and #108): the bot climbed out of its pit on a pillar of its own cobblestone and stood eight blocks from the only trunk in reach, across two blocks of air the path search does not bridge, and waited `:no-log` (`a-trunk-across-a-chasm-is-bridged-by-stairs-up`).
- A trunk its own leaves wall in is tunnelled to. `trees-tunnel`, after `trees-bridge` in `climb-out`: out of the ground (or on from a run just ended, `tunneled?`, which also keeps `trees-bridge` from cutting a staircase beside it), a standing trunk within `bridge-reach` on the plane and 12 of the eye, further than `tunnel-reach` (2) from the feet, no open water on the way, not given up (`:plan/blacklist`, two failures in its column), never from a boxed cell, gets a `:tunnel-to` (`tunnel-steps` 24, `:intent/dry?`, `:intent/for :trees`) that ends beside it, where `near-walk` has a route. A refused step fails the run `:blocked`, and `plan/fail-intent` gives the trunk up. It is not skipped for a failed walk's neighbourhood (`:plan/unreached`): that failure is this one. A staircase down toward such a trunk ended two blocks short and alternated with the bridge's staircase up. The collect after it digs the leaf under the feet (`intent/leaf-under`) for a log that fell into the hole beneath the canopy the bot stands on, and walks to within `centre-tolerance` of a drop below the feet so a bot on a leaf beside the hole falls in. Sim world R1/4 (issues #370, #186) and real-2/1 (issue #87) (`a-trunk-walled-in-by-its-leaves-is-tunnelled-to`, `the-leaf-under-the-feet-is-a-blocker-of-a-drop-below-it`, `a-collect-centres-over-a-drop-below-the-feet`).
- A failed `:cross` blacklists nothing. Its target is the trunk across the water, and a failed swim is not a failed trunk: `plan/fail-intent` counts it against the trunk's column in `:plan/trunk-failures` (two give the trunk up, as for a log's walk) and leaves it among `climb-out`'s candidates, where `trees-bridge` climbs toward it. Sim worlds R3/45 (issues #87 and #108) crossed a lake toward the only trunk in reach, met the foot of a cliff with the trunk on its top and failed `:stuck`; the blacklisted trunk left the stairs up the cliff nothing to climb toward (`a-failed-crossing-keeps-its-trunk-for-the-bridge`).
- The tag exists so a walk for another purpose (to a crafting table) is never continued as a dig; before it, any finished walk or dig was assumed to be part of a log chain.
- The chain lives entirely in `:plan/last` and the intent maps; nothing else remembers it. A failed intent clears `:plan/last`, so a failure restarts the chain from a fresh search with the target blacklisted.
- The sim test drops a 2x2 oak canopy one block above the ground between bot and trunk; the bot breaks the leaf at `[3 65 0]` and finishes with no failed attempts.
- The near band is 12 above and below the eye, so from a mine every surface tree is past it. Stairs are cut only once a trunk is remembered, no route reaches it, and the shaft already dug cannot be walked up. Full run 29ef069 cut one at spawn before any tree was remembered, failed `:stair-not-taken`, and the plan then waited `:no-log`. Full run 4528031 remembered the trees, found no route through the mine, cut a new stair from y 17, and the only pickaxe broke at y 49 (`a-shaft-to-the-sky-is-walked-not-cut`). `:mine-iron` keeps a spare stone pickaxe and gathers the wood for it while one still digs.
- Being out of the ground is not being at the trees. Race 38674091115 runner 4 (issue #87): the iron mine left the bot at the mouth of its shaft under the open sky at y 70 with 999 trunks remembered and the nearest thirty blocks above on the mountain; the near band stops twelve over the eye, no walk climbs the cliff, the hunt's targets stop sixteen over the eye and never come nearer the landing, and the climb refused to cut because `stairs/out?` said out, so the plan waited `:no-log`. `trees-climb` cuts stairs up toward a trunk remembered above the eye no walk reaches, choosing the cardinal heading that reduces the larger horizontal distance (x on a tie) — `:intent/to-height?` only, since the sky over the feet ends the plain climb on its first tick — and the boxed-cell guard still bounds it.
- The mirror: a trunk far below the eye (past the near band's twelve), out of the ground, no walk reaching it, is descended toward with `:stairs-down` (`wood/trees-descend`, within `descend-reach` on the plane, never from a boxed cell). Real race (issue #57, snapshot real-4-s2-Clj_r07): bot on a mountain top at y 238, nearest trunk 56 below and 60 across, waited `:no-log`; it now cuts stairs down toward it.
  Once a length is cut the next goes on from its foot (`wood/descended?`: the last intent a done `:stairs-down` `:intent/for :trees`), in the ground, where `stairs/out?` no longer holds: from the foot the walk up the air already dug (`intent/opening`) took the bot back to the mouth every seven seconds to cut the same length again. Sim race 39593219246 (issue #135, world R4/4) did it for fifteen minutes with the trunks forty below and the food at zero (`the-next-length-down-goes-on-from-the-foot-of-the-last`).

- The climb out never pillars up in the air to find the trees. Sim snapshots R1/4 (issue #186) and R2/29, R4/16 (issue #89), and real-2 s2 Clj_r15 (issue #186) each ended on a pillar of the bot's own steps in the open air, ten to twenty over a sea, a valley or a cliff, with the trunks twenty to sixty blocks off and no route to any; the stairs of a climb out had gone east whichever way the trees lay, the water's surface had been climbed from instead of crossed, and the floors of a way back down are refused over water (`a-floor-is-never-laid-over-water`). Now: the climb out is cut `toward` the nearest trunk (`:intent/dir`, as `trees-climb`'s is; `a-climb-out-is-cut-toward-the-trunk`); a bot afloat under the open sky `crossing`s before it climbs (`a-bot-in-the-water-swims-for-the-trees-before-it-climbs`); a stair down for the trees or a valley lays its floor over water (`stairs/bridging?`, `a-descent-toward-the-trees-lays-its-floor-over-water`), the descent ending at the water where the crossing takes over; and the gather act's last resort before the wait, `valley` (`gather-or-out`, never `gather-next`, so a spare is not sent), cuts stairs down toward the nearest trunk whatever its height from a perch no walk, hunt or crossing leaves (`a-perch-across-a-valley-is-descended-for-the-trees`), on from the foot of each length while it is in the ground and no deeper than `valley-floor` under the trunk (`descended-valley?`, `a-valley-goes-on-from-the-foot-of-its-last-length`: the walk up the air already dug took the bot back to the perch every few seconds), the trunk then above the eye and `trees-climb`'s.
- A grove no route reaches is one failure. `plan/fail-intent` adds the target of a log walk that failed `:no-path` to `:plan/unreached`, and `wood/skipped` passes over every log within `unreached-radius` (8) of one, so the far-candidates are not twelve trunks of one grove, a walk each (twelve unwalkable walks fail the plan). Real race 39042120284 bot Clj_r14 (issue #75, snapshot real-1-s1) stood on a shore with the trees two hundred blocks off across the sea; the hunt and the crossing never had their turn.
- A descent ends in the ground. A stair down for the trees (or a valley) stops once the trunk is within `continue-drop` of the eye, and the foot of it is not out of the ground (`stairs/out?`), so `climb-out` cut stairs up to the top of the world, the bot walked down its own stairs, and the descent was cut again for the whole window (issue #135, snapshot R2-43-1). From the foot of such a descent (`descended?`, `descended-valley?`) or on from a run (`tunneled?`), `trees-tunnel` runs level through the rock to the trunk within `foot-reach` (40) instead of `bridge-reach` (`a-descent-that-ends-level-with-the-trunk-tunnels-on`).
- A perch under the sky is not a climb to the top of the world. `perched?` is under the sky, not out, not a pit (`pit-rim`) and not a cell a climb died `:boxed` in. From there `climb-out` descends before it walks the opening or cuts stairs up to `terrain/top-y`: `trees-descend` when a trunk is more than `continue-drop` under the eye (the descent is allowed from a perch, not only when out), else `perch-down`, one `stair-depth` toward the nearest trunk at or below the feet. A pit and a trunk above the feet still climb out. Sim race 39934917165 (issue #89, world R1/23) spent its dirt on that tower and boxed on the way down (`a-perch-under-the-sky-descends-toward-a-lower-trunk`).
