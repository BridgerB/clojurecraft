# Stairs down

`{:intent/kind :stairs-down :intent/to-y n}` (optionally `:intent/dir [dx dz]`) cuts a staircase in its heading until the feet are at or below `to-y`. It never digs the block under the feet: a blind drop is how the siblings' bots died ([[sib-dig-down-safety]]).

## Key files
- `stairs.clj`, `stair-cells` - the three cells a stair ahead opens: head-up, head, and the new feet one block down.
- `stairs.clj`, `exposed` - what the stair exposes to the body: those three, the floor under the new feet, and every horizontal neighbour of the three.
- `stairs.clj`, `clear-way?` - none of the opened cells water, lava or unloaded; no lava in any exposed cell. A new feet cell in `:plan/boxed-at` (where a staircase died `:boxed`) is refused. `safe?` is that and a floor under the new feet.
- `stairs.clj`, `lay` - where the floor is missing and the way is clear: the cells to lay it from a carried filler (`fillers`: cobblestone, the stones that drop themselves, dirt, then netherrack, what a Nether descent digs: gym batch pearls-warped-e run 3 had every stair refused in a pocket with no filler held) (`floor-laying`: the floor cell when a solid block borders it to place against, `place/support`; else a holder under the block under the feet first, placed against that block's underside, then the floor against the holder; never a floor over water or lava, `over-fluid?`: the first block under it within `bridge-depth`, 16, that is not air is a fluid, and the floor would be a bridge. Sim race 38657453715 world R4/1 (issue #62) cut stairs down a mountain onto a lake, and its level steps laid a floor against the last one twenty-six blocks out over the water until the cobblestone was spent; every stair from that causeway was `:boxed`, and it walked back and forth along it for 75 minutes until the run's time ran out, `a-floor-is-never-laid-over-water`), when `filler` finds enough of one (`fillers`: cobblestone, the stones that drop themselves, dirt; never gravel or sand). A stair up is cut only from dry feet (`up-dry?`): from feet standing in water fed from beside them (`wet-feet`) a jump only bobs, never a block up onto the step, so the water cells beside the feet are filled first (`dry-feet`, in `up-lay` after the seal) and the water under the feet drains (race 38988094276 runner 3 bot Clj_r05, issue #320: the portal build's stairs up from its own flooded tunnel bobbed to y 41.36 and failed `:stair-not-taken` three times; `a-stair-up-is-not-cut-from-feet-in-fed-water`). A stair up lays its step instead (`step-lay`, `shapes`): the block ahead at the feet's height, which its new feet cell stands on (`step-to-lay`: the step alone when supported, else a holder under it beside the current floor first, then the step), and only for a stair the dig would then cut (nothing falling over the head, `falls-over?`; no opened cell bordering a fluid, `intent/exposes-fluid?`). Both go through `cells-to-lay`: the way clear, enough of a filler carried.
- `stairs.clj`, `onto` - the controls that walk onto the new feet cell; `creep-speed` 0.06.
- `intent.clj`, `feet` - y a touch below the position, since physics jitter dips it under the integer.
- `intent.clj`, `run-child` - the child `:dig` (`intent/child-dig`) stands in as `:plan/intent` for one tick of the dig executor, then returns to `:intent/child`: intents composing intents, with one rule for breaking blocks ([[dig-timeline]]).
- `stairs.clj`, `max-turns` 4, `step-timeout-ticks` 200.

## How it works
For a descent, `down-safe?` also checks the solid cells against the child dig's fluid refusals; already open cells need no dig. `down-lay` first tries the same side-water seal as a climb (`seal`), then the missing floor (`lay`). A dry heading still comes before a seal (`rock-heading`). A seal over an existing floor costs only its own blocks, not the cave-floor budget (`floors-affordable?`), but must still leave `lay-reserve` for the climb out. Climbs can spend that reserve. The refusals for lava, water overhead, and unsupported placements are unchanged. Race 39007359500 runner 5 bot Clj_r06 (issue #342) turned from side water until it stood on a dry shelf with no route away, five cobblestone and twenty dirt held, and waited `[:furnace :boxed]`. Sealing lets it descend into rock and mine again. Race 39070889905 runner 1 bot Clj_r15 then spent its remaining filler on descent seals and could not seal its escape climb. Keeping the reserve lets that snapshot climb out, mine cobblestone and craft the furnace 127.4 seconds after its moment.

Each stage is a method of `stairs-stage`; `run :stairs-down` only checks whether the feet are down far enough.
Refusals belong to the current feet cell: returning from `:step` to `:plan` resets `:intent/turns`, so a completed stair does not spend the next cell's headings. Race 39037024176 runner 1 (issue #342) carried three refusals through successful stairs and boxed before considering the other directions from its new footing. This also recovers a saved staircase already carrying those refusals.
1. `:plan`: with the body in the air, neither on the ground nor in a liquid (`airborne?`), wait: round falling feet every cell is air and every heading would be refused for the drop (race 38477665442, landing 2361,111,2361, issue #65: a walk to stone stepped off a cliff and the staircase the mine started on the way down was `:boxed` three times in the four seconds of the fall; `a-staircase-started-in-the-air-waits-for-the-ground`; race 38515209959 bots Clj_r04 and Clj_r06, issue #107, had a climb out asked for mid-jump against a two-high wall, its cells a block too high and the jump meeting the wall until `:stair-not-taken`, `a-stair-up-asked-for-mid-jump-waits-for-the-ground`). Else, with the current heading (`+x` first), compute the stair; `safe?` → `:open`; else, the way clear but the floor missing and a filler carried (`lay`) → `:lay`, for a stair down only while the filler carried would floor the rest of the way to `:intent/to-y` at this stair's rate and keep `lay-reserve`, 8, for the climb back (`floors-affordable?`; race 38984748832 runner 3 bot Clj_r01, issue #37: stairs down broke into a cave at y 50 with 16 cobblestone and 25 dirt, floored thirty-one stairs down through it and stood at y 19 on a one-block dirt bridge with nothing left, every stair refused, `:boxed`; `a-stair-down-lays-floors-only-while-the-filler-reaches-its-depth`), else it turns as for any refusal; else turn a quarter and count. Four refusals → `step-aside`: a level step (`level-cells`: head and feet one block along, same height) that `level-safe?` allows (a stair's rules, and no opened cell bordering a fluid, which the child dig would refuse), then `:plan` again from there with the turns counted afresh. `level-safe?` also refuses a cell too hard for the held tools (`too-hard?`: a tool of its kind held, none that drops it), which the child dig would refuse `:needs-tool`: gym batch iron-a9 run 4's tunnel at y 2 met such ore and failed three times in a moment (`a-tunnel-goes-round-ore-its-pickaxe-cannot-drop`). The first takes the first heading allowed; each later one keeps the last one's way (`:intent/level-dir`) while it is allowed and never goes straight back, so the staircase walks out from under what refuses it. Going down, the headings are ranked by `floor-distance`, how many columns along each the first lies where a stair's floor would stand (the cell two under the feet's height solid, or clear with no fluid within `bridge-depth` under it), within `shore-reach`: in a cave over a lake sixteen blocks down every stair is refused for the water under its floor while a level step, one block higher, may lay its own, and gym batch stronghold-room2-a run 3 (issue #265) crept 32 the way its last turn faced and was `:boxed` with the lake's shore 33 off, where batch stronghold-room-a's run 3 had found its stair on the 33rd step (`a-descent-over-a-cave-lake-steps-toward-the-nearest-floor`). Going up, every step is ranked instead (see Stairs up), the last way first on a tie, and a way whose next cell is not under gravel or sand (`under-falling?`) comes before any that is, whatever the last way: under a bed of gravel every stair is refused again from the next cell, and race 38477665442 bot Clj_r04 stepped thirty cells along one at y 42 until its last pickaxe broke (issue #43, `a-climb-steps-out-from-under-a-bed-of-gravel-not-along-it`). No heading allows one, or `level-step-limit` is spent (`max-level-steps`, 32; for a climb as many more as the landing is far on the plane: gym batch iron-a9 run 1 climbed under the sea 155 blocks from its landing and was `:boxed` after 32 steps three times; for a descent with a stair's floor in sight along some heading, `shore-reach` more): fail `:boxed`. When each step took the first heading allowed, live run 9 (cutting stairs up under water at y 51) stepped round a square of four cells until it failed `:boxed` three times (`stairs-up-walk-out-from-under-water`).
2. `:lay`: lay the floor's cells (`:intent/lay`, the first not yet solid at a time), each a child `:place` of the filler at a given cell (`:intent/cell`, as a build places; [[place-intent]]), then `:open` (or `:level` for a level step). A placement the server refused, or no filler left, refuses the stair like any cell that cannot be cut (`refuse`: turn, or `step-aside` when boxed in). `step-aside` takes a level step that can be laid when none is `level-safe?` (`level-clear?` and `lay`), and `deeper` in the mine act sends stairs down where one can be cut or cast ([[mine-act]]). No lay is planned that would step the body off its footing (`backs-off?`): a cell the body clips makes the place step toward the feet cell's middle first (`place/nudge-off`), and when that cell is `unfooted?` (no floor in the `edge-drop`, 2, cells under it, or water or lava the first thing under it, `over-fluid?`) the body stands on a neighbour's edge and the step walks it off; the stair turns instead. Gym batch pearls-warped8-c (plan run 39845223845, probe 39854206523) run 2 stood on a nylium ledge's edge over a lava lake eleven blocks down, a stair up toward the ledge laid its step where the body stood, and the nudge walked it into the lava (`:no-footing`; `a-stair-lays-nothing-that-steps-the-body-off-an-edge`).
3. `:open`: while any of the three cells is solid, run a child dig of the topmost. A child that finds its cell already open (`:target-gone`, `fell?`) is as good as done: gravel or sand fell out of it. A child refused for one of `refusals` turns the stair like an unsafe plan: `:needs-tool` (ore the pickaxe cannot drop) or `:unsafe` (a cell beside water or lava, which the dig executor will not open, since it would let the fluid in). A child the server did not do (`:rejected`: its ack came with the block still standing, [[dig-timeline]]) leaves the cell to open, so it is dug again (`rejected`, counted in `:intent/rejections`; `max-rejections`, 3, fail the staircase `:rejected`): race 38477665442 runner 9 (issues #59, #66), a lagging server with 25 bots dropped early FINISHes queued behind one another, and the staircase, believing the ack, walked into rock the server still had there until the step timed out (`a-stair-the-server-did-not-cut-is-dug-again`). Any other failure fails the stair with its reason.
4. `:level`: dig the level step's cells with child digs, then walk until the feet are in its feet cell; 10 s from the step's start without that fails `:stair-not-taken`. A child dig refused (`refusals`: `:unsafe`, `:needs-tool`) after the step was chosen does not fail it (`level-refused`): a tunnel plans its next step again from where the body stands (`tunnel-plan`, which now refuses what the world shows), a staircase turns (`refuse`). Sim race 38762695024 runner 8 (issue #171, world R1/40, every seed): a tunnel for gravel at y 40 ran under a gravel bed; the gravel over each step it opened fell into the step round the body, water came down onto it, and the dig to clear the step was refused `:unsafe`; three tunnels failed in eight seconds and the run ended `[:flint-and-steel :unsafe]` (`a-tunnel-step-the-dig-refuses-is-planned-again`).
5. `:step`: walk onto the new feet cell (`onto`); done with the stair when the feet are half a block below where it began, which is the only proof the server broke what the bot asked for, and centred in the cell (`taken?`, `intent/centred?`). Going down where nothing beyond the cell stops the body (the cell past it is not solid: a cave, a floor laid over it all there is to land on), the walk creeps: forward only on the ground and under `creep-speed`, so the body leaves the edge slowly and lands on the cell; into rock the wall ahead stops it, and going up the jump needs the push. A stair cell solid again (gravel fell into it) goes back to `:open`; 10 s without either fails `:stair-not-taken`.
6. Done when the feet are at or below `to-y`.

## Stairs up
A climb out to the surface (`:intent/for :surface`) with no pickaxe held and a filler carried is a shaft (`shaft?`, stage `:shaft`), not stairs: the cell over the head is dug by hand (`shaft-cell`, only when `shaft-safe?`) and a child `:pillar` lays a block under the feet, one dig a level instead of three. A stair up dug by hand is about 30 s a level, so the hundred levels from the deepslate outlasted the judged window (sim snapshots R2-36-1 and R3-13-1, issue #135; R2-45-1, issue #178). A child that fails gives the shaft up for the intent (`:intent/no-shaft`, `unshaft`) and the stairs resume.
An interrupted duck may be reconsidered only with solid floor directly under the feet cell, not while the body still overlaps the previous floor's edge. Otherwise the new ascent is planned a block above where the feet will land. A rising `:step` that falls more than half a block below its launch height releases its controls and replans from the ground instead of jumping against a step two blocks high (issue #368, R4/20 seed 1).
An ascent may regain a previously visited higher cell: height gained is progress, while a descent and level steps still avoid revisiting cells, and `clear-way?` still refuses `:plan/boxed-at`. A duck already in flight is reconsidered on the ground when a dry stair up that the held tool can cut is available, before its child digs away that stair's step. Sim race 39024341119 runner 3 (issue #363, R1/35 seed 1) ducked through its own ledge and was `[:portal :boxed]` with hundreds of filler blocks held. Regaining the step and laying supported steps out from the ledge lets the saved moment recover, replace its worn-out pickaxe and descend to y -56; the later walk to the site remains a separate no-path problem.
`{:intent/kind :stairs-up :intent/to-y n}` is the same staircase climbed (`shapes`: each kind's cells, rule and rise). A stair up opens `up-cells`: the cell over the head, which the jump needs, then the new head and the new feet one block up and ahead; the block ahead at the feet's height is the step. It is cut under `up-safe?`: `safe?`'s rules, and no gravel or sand over the cell above the head, which would fall through it onto the bot (`gravel-over-the-head-is-never-dug-under`: every heading refused, a level step out from under it). `:step` walks into the step, which jumps; `taken?` going up also needs the feet on the ground, since halfway up a jump the feet are already half a block higher and the next stair was once planned from the air. A climb the planner starts (the scoop's way out, the gather's) aims `climb-stairs` (8) up at a time (`climb-to`), as a descent cuts `stone/stair-depth`, so the planner looks again between lengths and makes the spare pickaxe while one still digs: race 38477665442 bots Clj_r23 and Clj_r06 climbed 26 and 53 stairs in one intent and wore both pickaxes out (issue #39). Done standing at `to-y`, or out of the ground (`out?`): under the open sky (`terrain/under-sky?`: nothing but air, plants or leaves over the feet to the top of the world) with a level step it can walk onto with nothing to dig, itself under the sky. The open sky alone is a pit: live run 11's stairs up stopped under the sky two below the ground, in a shaft one wide, and no route led out to water (`stairs-up-end-on-the-ground-not-in-a-shaft`). A level step alone is a pit's floor too: `pit-rim` looks `rim-reach` (8) columns out along each heading for the first the feet could not step into (`steppable?`: no cell to stand in a block down, level or a block up, and a block up only with the jump's headroom clear over the feet, `step-rises`: one stair out of R2/45's crevice stood in a notch under the leaves that every column round was standable a block up from, and the notch passed for open ground) and takes it for the pit's edge that way, a wall when ground lies over the feet in it (`ground-over`, within `climb-stairs` up, logs and leaves not counted) or a drop; a pit has an edge every way and at least one wall, and `out?` is false in one, so the climb goes on to the rim. Race 38515209959 (issues #102, #103, 30 real bots) and the sim race in worlds R3/36 and R3/32 stood at the bottom of the quarry the stone mine had dug round their feet, two or three deep and a few cells wide, the stairs they came down by eaten by the digs; the sky over the feet and a step across the floor said out, and each waited `:no-log` (`a-quarry-with-a-level-step-across-its-floor-is-climbed-out-of`, `a-log-is-gathered-out-of-a-quarry`). A plain climb now leaves `open-pit` too (`a-climb-from-a-pit-ends-at-its-height-not-at-the-open-sky`). Every level step of a climb takes the heading nearest the shore (`shore-distance`: the first column within `shore-reach`, 48, whose top block is solid rather than a fluid, `dry-top?`), then the one along which the head is soonest dry (`dry-head-distance`: the first loaded column with no fluid at the head's height up to the cell over the one the jump needs, `wet-head?`, y + 1 to y + 3), then the one toward the landing, which is land, since what refuses a climb is most often a lake or the sea overhead: gym batch iron-a7 run 1 climbed to y 44 under a lake (stone, sand, then water to y 63 over the head), stepped 32 east under the lake and failed `:boxed` (`stairs-up-under-a-lake-step-toward-the-shore`). Ice is a lake's own top, not its shore (`lake-ice`): sim race 38659291345 world R4/38 (issue #37) climbed under a lake half frozen over and took each patch of ice for the shore, stepping toward it every way in turn. Under an aquifer the top of every column is dry ground and the shore says nothing; the sheet over the head does. Issue #61 (sim race 38515210080, world R3/6, 104 runs a round, 44 real bots over three races): a climb for water from y 37 stood under an aquifer whose floor was two blocks over its head and over its own mine; every stair up was refused, `:unsafe` for the water over the cell it opened or for the step missing under its new feet, and the level steps went north under the aquifer and along the mine, a floor laid under each, until `:boxed` (the sheet ended three columns south). `stairs-up-under-an-aquifer-step-out-from-under-it` and `stairs-up-lay-their-step-across-a-cave` hold both halves. The cells a staircase's level steps stand on are kept (`:intent/visited`); a level step onto one comes after every other way, and no descent goes back onto one: R4/38's climb stepped round a patch of fifteen cells under the lake a hundred times, one fresh step always allowed, until `:boxed`. A climb with no fresh level step ducks (`duck`) before it is `:boxed`: a stair down (`:intent/rise -1` for that step) along the heading whose head is dry soonest, of those cut clear of water and lava, the pocket's feet cell added to `:plan/boxed-at`. Under the sheet the level steps are allowed again. The same round's world R1/33 stood in a pocket two cells long with an aquifer's sheet a block over the head, every stair up and every level step bordering water, and failed `:boxed` three times; the duck took it under the sheet and back to its mine. The scoop's `climb` cuts these when no route leads back to the landing ([[scoop-intent]]). A climb out to the surface (`:intent/for :surface`, the water hunt's and the gather's) that is `:boxed` ends rather than failing, its feet cell put in `:plan/boxed-at` (`box`, issue #61), and its act chooses something else from there. A cut toward the bank of water no route reaches (`:intent/for :bank`, `scoop/to-bank`) that is `:boxed` ends too, giving up the water it headed for (`:intent/target`, put in `:plan/blacklist`; issue #61, [[scoop-intent]]). A climb out of a pit under the sky (`scoop/pit-climb`, issue #60) says `:intent/to-height? true`: it ends at `to-y` alone, since a ravine's floor under the sky is `out?` the tick it starts (`a-climb-from-a-pit-ends-at-its-height-not-at-the-open-sky`).

## Tunnels
`{:intent/kind :tunnel :intent/dir d :intent/length n}` is level steps alone: `tunnel-plan` takes the next along its heading when `level-safe?`, else a step to one side, keeping the heading, so the tunnel goes round what refused it; the side is the first of the two allowed and then the only one (`:intent/side`), so it never steps back and forth; `:boxed` when neither the heading nor that side allows a step. It never turns back down itself: when it turned a quarter at each refusal, live run 8 (refused east at y 16) tunnelled west beside its own tunnel and gym batch iron-a2 run 4 walked its tunnel back and forth for eight minutes (`a-tunnel-goes-round-what-refuses-it`). The `:level` stage is the staircase's own. Done after n steps; a tunnel toward a bank (`:intent/for :bank`, `scoop/to-bank`) that ends no nearer its water on the plane than it began (`:intent/from-feet`, `no-nearer?`, by `nearer-by` 1) gives that water and the sources round it (`pond-sources`, within `pond-reach` 6) up into `:plan/blacklist`: sim race 38839767273 runner 6 (issue #69, worlds R3/8 and R4/35 at 3f836ff) walked an open corridor past its water, its heading turning as it passed, eight steps east and eight back, two thousand tunnels until the run's ninety minutes were spent (`a-bank-tunnel-that-comes-no-nearer-gives-the-pond-up`). Not when a walk to the bank has a route at the tunnel's end (`intent/routable?`): the tunnel opened the way. Sim world R4/30 seed 1 (issue #69's second round, ef4e73b against 5e93cae) gave up a pond its tunnel had just opened the walk to, and tunnelled on to other water. The mine act cuts one at a row's depth ([[mine-act]]).

**Planks and the mouth of a hole (issues #37, #130).** `filler` falls back to a planks item when no filler of `fillers` is held; `carried-filler` counts none, so a descent's reserve never spends them, but a climb's seal or step can be laid from them (a bot on a strip over air held only planks and sticks). `dry-feet` lays the water beside wet feet, and a cell of them with no solid neighbour (the mouth of a water-filled hole) is laid after a water cell beside it that has one: the helpers come first in the list and the lay stage places in order.

## Gotchas
- **A boxed tunnel for lava ends instead of failing.** `box`: with `:intent/for :lava` and a target it adds the feet to `:plan/boxed-at`, gives the source up and ends done, as a bank tunnel gives its water up. See [[build-plan]].
- **Rock before a laid floor.** A stair down whose heading would need a floor laid first turns to the first other heading whose stair is cut into rock as it stands (`rock-heading`), and lays only when none is: race 38842339863 runner 4 (issue #37) bots Clj_r01 and Clj_r08 ran east along a cliff's face and a cave's wall, a floor before every stair with the rock a turn away west, until the few cobblestone the furnace left were spent and every stair was `:boxed` (`a-stair-down-turns-into-the-rock-before-it-lays-a-floor`). A climb, a level way and a cut toward a bank keep their heading.
- Keys that are over (`:intent/child`, `:intent/cells`) are removed, never nil: the world spec refuses a nil vector.
- Water beside a stair is allowed, lava is not: the siblings found refusing water wedged descents in wet biomes. A cell that itself borders water is still never dug (the dig's `exposes-fluid?`): the stair turns instead. Before it turned, a live iron-kit descent (local landing 2360,2360) met an aquifer at y 36 and failed `:unsafe` three times in a moment, the planner restarting the same refused stair (`water-beside-a-stair-cell-turns-the-stairs`). Going up, water beside a stair's cells is sealed first: `seal` (the shape's `:lay` for `:stairs-up`, under `up-sealed?`, which adds to `up-safe?` that no cell borders a fluid) lays a carried filler into each water cell among the four horizontal neighbours of the cells (`water-beside`; a block placed into a source takes its place, [[sim-placement]]), when each has a solid neighbour to place against, the cell is not inside the player (`place/inside-player?`: the server rejects a placement into the body; batch chain-a2 run 1 swam onto a bank with water at its stand, and every stair tried to seal the water at its own feet, `:rejected`, `:boxed`), and the water is beside alone (`water-over?`: water straight over or under a cell, or on gravel over it, is a lake overhead, which the level steps still walk out from under toward the shore). Race 38477665442 runner 8 bot Clj_r01 (issue #54) climbed out of its mine with the bucket through an aquifer: water beside every stair cell, each child dig refused `:unsafe`, the staircase `:boxed` twice, and twelve walks to that water failed `:no-path` (`stairs-up-through-an-aquifer-are-sealed-with-cobblestone`: six stairs up through a ring of sources, no water let in).
- **A lake under every stair is stepped around.** The fifth live iron-kit run (local landing 3024,2360) stood at y 52 over an underground lake filling y 48-51: every stair's new feet had water under or beside them, and the staircase failed `:boxed` three times in a moment. A level step leads along until a stair is safe; `boxed-in-by-lava-on-every-side-fails-boxed` now needs lava beside the feet too. Full run ab3945e reached a furnace and a stone pickaxe, then stood at y 32 where every stair and every further level step was refused; two more staircases from that cell boxed in under a second. `fail-intent` blacklists the cell, the mine does not start another staircase there (`a-boxed-descent-is-not-started-again-from-the-same-cell`), and `safe?` will not step back onto it.
- **A cave under the stairs is cast through.** Race 38477665442 runner 8 (issue #37, 29 real bots and 1,335 sim runs a round `[:ingots :boxed]`): five of its eight bots that stopped `:boxed` stood on the lip of a cave their staircase had broken into, 7 to 284 cobblestone in hand, every stair refused for the drop under it; and from the lip nothing else could be cut (`stairs` refuses a cell in `:plan/boxed-at`, no tunnel, no deeper stair, no retreat), so the mine waited `:boxed` out. The floor is now laid first (`lay`): `a-cave-under-the-stairs-is-cast-through-from-carried-cobblestone` casts five stairs down from one block of stone over a cave, ten blocks laid and nothing dug, and with nothing to lay from is `:boxed` as before. Two more of the eight stood in aquifers (water beside every stair and level step), which laying does not address.
- **A stair ends centred, or it is a shaft.** Clj_r18's recording (the same race) showed each stair's feet in the column before the stair's, standing on the edge of the cell's floor: the body had landed past the cell's middle, and the next stair, planned from the column it stood in, dug the floor it stood on. The staircase went straight down a shaft one block wide, which no route led back up. `taken?` now needs the feet centred in the new cell, and `onto` walks them there; pushed all the way, the model left the lip at 0.11 blocks a tick, 0.19 in the air, and landed at the far edge of the laid floor, then fell off it, which is why the walk creeps where nothing stops the body.
- **A level step seals the water beside the cells it digs.** Sim races 39081494214 R1/31 and 39074159590 R3/44 (issue #105, `[:flint-and-steel :boxed]`, 63 runs): a climb up under a lake for the water bucket let the lake into its own shaft, and the bot stood in it with 306 and 514 cobblestone, water beside the cells of every level step (`intent/exposes-fluid?` refuses a dig that borders a fluid), the stairs `:boxed` three times in a second and the plan with them. `step-aside` now also takes a step `level-lay` allows: the water beside the cells it digs (`fluids-beside-dug`, the six neighbours of each `dug-cells`, solid ones only: an open cell needs no dig) is filled first with a carried filler, each water cell with a solid neighbour to place against, then the floor the step lacks (`floor-laying`), and the step is cut as any other. Open steps with water beside them are floored and walked, not dug. Lava beside, the cells the body stands in, ore too hard for the tools, a fluid over gravel above a cell and a floor over water still refuse it (`a-level-step-seals-the-water-beside-the-cells-it-digs`).
- **A descent's level step keeps the climb's reserve.** `reserve-level-lay`: a level step of a stair down lays its floor (`level-lay`) only when `lay-reserve` filler stays, as `lay` does for the stairs. `level-lay` is its own path with no reserve, so a descent over a void that `lay` refused at the reserve floored fifteen level steps of a bridge with the same dirt and three planks, to none (real race 39788962719 runner 7, Clj_r16, issue #37, snapshot real-7-s2), and every stair up from the bridge's end was `:boxed` (`a-descents-level-step-keeps-the-climbs-reserve`). A stair up's step is what the reserve is for and lays as before. The reserve is `bridge-reserve`: `lay-reserve`, or the open air over the step's feet cell (`void-rise`, at most `max-void-reserve`, 16) when that is more, since a climb out of a void pillars a block a level, and eight carried a bridge only eight levels toward a roof ten up (the same bot after the first fix climbed from y 10 to 15 and was `:boxed` again).
- **Every floor a descent lays keeps the way back.** `floor-reserve`: `lay`, which a descent's stair floors, its level steps (`step-aside`'s `laid`, before `reserve-level-lay`) and a row's `deeper` all pass, keeps `bridge-reserve` (`lay-reserve`, or the open air over the new feet cell up to `max-void-reserve`) rather than `lay-reserve` alone; a stair up keeps `lay-reserve`. `reserve-level-lay` only guarded the wet variant, because `step-aside` took `lay`'s answer first. Real bot Clj_r16 (issue #37, snapshot real-7-s2) floored eleven levels and a bridge of six cells with sixteen dirt, kept eight, and pillared back from y 10 with five. With the larger reserve it lays no floor over a deep void it cannot climb back out of, and the descent turns to the rock. A tall cave now wants up to sixteen filler for a floor; `a-descents-level-step-keeps-the-climbs-reserve` pins it.
- **A climb takes the step already there before it lays one.** In `:plan`, a stair up whose step is missing toward the current heading but stands toward another (`fresh-safe?` over the other three, a quarter turn at a time) is cut toward that one: the stairs a descent laid are climbed back the way it came, the bot at the foot of its own bridge with eight blocks (real bot Clj_r16, issue #37) having laid two a rise toward the heading it began with.
- **A boxed climb pillars up.** `box`, the last of `step-aside`, goes up as a shaft instead of failing `:boxed` when `pillar-up?`: a stair up, a `pillar-item` held (a filler, else planks, else gravel or sand, which stay where they are put on a block though they fall out of a floor over air), a solid block under the centred feet and the cells over the head safe to dig (`shaft-safe?`), the shaft not given up before (`:intent/no-shaft`). The `:shaft` stage is the existing one (a child `:dig`, then the `:pillar` intent dragon.clj defines, called as it is). Sim R3/15 seeds 1 and 2 (issue #37, #531): the bot on mineshaft planks at y -1 over open air holding 38 gravel had every stair and level step refused for the floor it lacked, and was `:boxed` (`a-boxed-climb-pillars-up-from-gravel`).
- **No pickaxe under a lake: bore up through the bed and swim.** Sim races (issues #69, #62, #161, #135; worlds R1/17, R3/45, R4/19, R3/30): four bots with the pickaxe broken stood in a pocket under a lake whose bed was stone, then gravel or sand, over fourteen to twenty-three blocks of water, and cut level steps toward a shore by hand all window (182 steps in a quarter of an hour, a block of stone 7500 ms) with 200 and more cells in `:plan/boxed-at`, which were a symptom: a cap on that set (the nearest sixty-four) was tried, broke `a-boxed-search-does-not-retreat-to-its-start` and could reopen the retreat ping-pong of issue #161 R1/29, and the bore alone ended every one of those stalls. `step-aside` of a climb now tries `bore` first when no pickaxe is held at all (`handed?`): `bore-cells` is one to `bore-bed` 3 blocks of rock over the head (from y + 2; gravel and sand count, and fall into the cell below as it is dug, to be dug again) under at most `bore-depth` 24 blocks of water that ends in open air, so the stage `:bore` digs them bottom first by hand (child digs with `:intent/exposes-ok?` and `:intent/drop-ok?`), then holds the jump under the shaft's centre, the water rising the bot at 2.7 blocks a second (nine seconds of the fifteen of breath at the deepest) and the swim-out taking over when it is trapped. With a pickaxe the level steps are quick and nothing changes. `scoop/bed-depth` is 3 too, so a bot that needs water scoops it from below through the same thickness.
- **A broken pickaxe stops ordinary descents.** Rock that drops only to a pickaxe with none held at all is not dug by hand going down, except toward trees: the stairs or tunnel fail `:no-pickaxe` (`tool-missing`) and the planner makes another. A stair up is cut by hand (`by-hand?`): the tool is made up top, so the way out is worth 7500 ms a block. Race 38477665442 Clj_r21 (issue #40): the pickaxe broke at y 60 in the iron mine, the stairs up stopped `:no-pickaxe`, the sticks for another were in the trees, and twelve walks to them failed `:no-path` (`stairs-up-with-no-pickaxe-are-cut-by-hand`). Batch iron-a2 runs went on by hand when the pickaxe broke, 7500 ms a stone for nothing, until a step timed out. A level step's 10 s is the walk's alone: it starts again after each dig. Only the pickaxe counts: a block that drops only to another tool is dug by hand in its own time, since the staircase wants the cell open, not the drop. Race 38477665442 (issues #42, #72): every bot landed in snow, on runners 2, 6 and 10, failed `:no-shovel` on its first stair, a snow block among its cells and a wooden pickaxe in hand (`snow-in-the-stair-is-cleared-by-hand`). `too-hard?` likewise asks `blocks/needs-tier`, the tier rule, not whether the block needs its tool.
- **The wood for a replacement can be below the feet.** A descent explicitly toward trees (`:intent/for :trees`) is also cut by hand (`by-hand?`), unlike a mining or lava descent. Issue #229's R4/5 moment held sticks and cobblestone but no table or pickaxe; it walked out to y 78 and repeatedly failed the descent toward wood `:no-pickaxe`. The saved moment now completes that descent and walks onward; `stairs-down-to-trees-with-no-pickaxe-are-cut-by-hand` also keeps ordinary descents tool-required.
- **A carpet is a cell to open.** `next-to-open` names a cell with any collision box (`blocks/collision-top`), not only a full cube: a moss carpet two over the feet, in the stair up's head cell, was called open, and the jump rose into its box three times for ten seconds each, `:stair-not-taken` (sim race 39593216223, issue #66, world R2/39; `a-carpet-over-the-head-is-a-cell-to-open-in-a-stair-up`).
- **Gravel falls into the stair.** The seventh live iron-kit run (local landing 2360,3000) cut its staircase through gravel at y 37-40: the gravel over the cells it opened fell into them, the walk onto the stair failed `:stair-not-taken`, then two digs found their cell already emptied and failed `:target-gone`, ending the plan. A cell refilled is dug again, a cell emptied counts as open, and the step goes back to digging; the server model drops gravel the way the game does ([[server-model]]), and `gravel-that-refills-a-stair-is-dug-again` cuts two stairs into a gravel cliff (it fails with the old stages). A falling block that lands on the bot's head is not handled.
- **A seal keeps what the climb back needs, not always eight.** `down-lay` seals the water beside a stair with `climb-reserve` kept: a block for each block the feet are under the highest rock (not leaves) of their column, up to `lay-reserve`, so a bot at the surface seals with all it has and one eight stairs down keeps all eight; a floor laid through a cave still keeps `lay-reserve` whatever the depth. Real bot Clj_r15 at R1/30 (issue #65) was one stair down from a lake shore with two dirt and every stair was refused for the eight (`a-seal-at-the-surface-keeps-nothing-for-a-climb-it-does-not-need`); the deeper reserve is race 39070889905's (issue #342).

- **A mine afloat swims out before it cuts stairs.** `stone/mine-next` asks `retreat` (a `:swim-out` for `:retreat` while `:player/in-water?`) before any stair or tunnel: sim race 39878751984 (issue #105, R1/11 seed 1) chased a mob into a lake and twice planned stairs down from the water, every one refused for its water cells and `:boxed` in a tick, which with a failed redig used the plan's three attempts and ended it at flint and steel (`a-mine-afloat-swims-out-before-it-cuts-stairs`).
