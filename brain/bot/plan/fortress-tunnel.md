# Fortress tunnel

The `:fortress` target (`goals.edn`, `:goal/dimension "minecraft:the_nether"`) is done when a fortress block on record (`nether_bricks`, its fence or stairs, at y 32 or more, so never one under the lava sea) is within 6 blocks of the feet. A `:tunnel-to` started with the feet off the ground waits up to `air-grace` 40 ticks (`:intent/airborne`) for them to land before it may fail `:blocked`: no step is safe from the air, and a bot knocked off a ledge failed three tunnels in three ticks (issue #502, R4-31-1). Its planner (`plan/next-intent :fortress`) starts a `:tunnel-to` (not stone's `:tunnel`, which digs along a direction for ore) to the nearest one on record, or, with none, the next leg of a spiral search (below).

`bricks` asks `memory/positions-now` for `brick-states`, the indexed current sightings remembered by the facts' identity, before filtering the height; it never scans all sightings with `memory/latest`. Issue #328's snapshot, once streamed chunks restored its dug room ([[server-model]]), could tunnel but exhausted the judge's four-minute budget in that full scan on every tick. The indexed query preserves the dimension and recency rules without repeating the whole-memory query.

## A step is a plan of ops
`step-plan feet move` is a vector of [[build-plan]] ops, a pure function of where the step starts and its `[dx dy dz]`: place a floor under the cell stepped into (it may be lava: a placement replaces it), dig the cells the body passes through (`opens`: the two it ends in, plus the head's cell on a step down or the cell over the head on a step up), and land near the middle of the new cell (`[:landed-centred to]`, the plan's goal). `build/next-op` and `build/work-op` work it exactly as a `:build` works the portal, so the dig, place and walk children are the ones the portal uses. The `:tunnel-to` intent chooses a move when a step starts (`:intent/move`, `:intent/from`), works the step until the bot stands in it, stops its controls, counts it, and stops at the target or after 32 steps, so the planner can choose again from what the new chunks showed.

Issue #339's real snapshot (`real-7-s1-Clj_r10`) ran past a descending step's repaired floor and fell into lava with no footing. A feet-cell match alone ended steps before centring or landing, leaving forward controls pressed; the walk also kept accelerating over a descending waypoint while waiting to fall to its height. The [[walk-intent]] stops horizontal input over that waypoint, and the tunnel waits for both centring and ground contact. In-flight steps re-read the same goal, replacing the older `[:feet to]` op rather than preserving its premature completion.

Issue #296 exhausted the last pickaxe and retried the same tunnel until three `:no-pickaxe` failures ended the plan. The fortress planner now makes a replacement when `build/pickaxe-at-hand?` finds a non-fetching producer, before starting another tunnel. An in-flight tunnel that loses its tool ends without spending a failed attempt, so that supply planning can run again. With no replacement at hand, the shared op executor lets a tunnel dig even slow rock by hand: the Nether may have no wood for a replacement, and quartz ore must not turn that state into an immediate dead end. Portal builds retain their hand-dig time limit; tunnel lava and protected-block rules are unchanged.

Issue #410's R1/34 and R2/39 snapshots spent every cobblestone floor block while carrying other safe filler, then failed `:not-held` three times. Before working a floor placement, the tunnel selects `stairs/filler` from the current inventory: cobblestone first, then the carried stones and dirt, never falling gravel or sand. A changed material changes the op, so `build/work-op` drops even an already failed placement child and equips the available filler; portal build materials and tunnel safety rules stay unchanged.

Issue #410's R2-17-1 and R2-12-1 failed `:not-held` with no filler in hand at all: a tunnel dug by hand drops nothing, and the floor placement fell back to `:cobblestone`. `tunnel-to` now gives the step up (its cell counted visited, so `next-move` takes another or the tunnel fails `:blocked` honestly) when a `:place` op has no `stairs/filler`, and `stairs/filler` lays `last-resorts` (bastion bricks, soul sand) after the stones and planks. A child op that fails `:rejected` (e.g. floor placement refused by server) similarly gives up that step and marks the cell visited, continuing to the next candidate move instead of failing `:tunnel-to` with `:rejected` (batch chain-a2 run 3). Issues #538 and #338 (R2-25-2, R2-6-2, R2-12-1) dug a zigzag down to the bedrock under bricks 34 to 47 blocks overhead: `moves` tried each direction's up, level and down in turn, so a level or down step in the first direction beat a safe step up in the next. A target farther above or below than it is aside takes the height first of every direction (`moves`). A brick on record is tunnelled to with `:intent/bridge? true`: the stalled-leg gate belonged to the exploring spiral, and a brick seen across a cavern (R2-22-2, 66 blocks east, 70 south over a basalt delta) was dug round at hand speed for the whole window. A brick within `shaft-aside` (8) blocks horizontally and more than `climb-past` above the feet is climbed as a shaft (`overhead?`, `shaft-tick`): the cell over the head dug by hand, then the stair's `:pillar` lays a block under the feet, one dig a level instead of a stair's three. R2-6-2 held no pickaxe at y 9 under a brick at y 56 and a stair reached y 43 when the window ended. A child that fails gives the shaft up for that tunnel (`:intent/no-shaft`) and the steps resume; lava over the head never starts one.

## Exploring: a spiral, not a line
With no brick on record the tunnel used to aim 48 blocks east, then 48 more, for ever, and drifted down a block whenever the level step was refused, into the bedrock. Sim race 39095704051 runner 4 (issue #338, world R4/33) went 630 blocks east along y 2-8 with a fortress 150 blocks north of its line; the snapshots of R2/4 and R3/21 stood in pockets of the bedrock layer or at the east edge of the captured region (the model's limit: nothing is loaded past it). `exploring` now returns a search, `:fortress/search` = {`:search/from` `:search/heading` `:search/leg` `:search/tunnels`}, and the planner's tunnel carries it (`:intent/search`; the `:tunnel-to` executor keeps it on the world, so an eat or a fight between two looks forgets nothing).

- **Legs of a square spiral.** The first leg goes east, each next one a quarter turn right (`turn-right`: east, south, west, north), `leg-length` blocks long: 160 for the first two (`explore-spacing`: a server shows 96 either side of the feet, `nether-view` 6 chunks), then each pair one spacing longer, so the strips swept lie a spacing apart and nothing is swept twice. The target is the leg's end (`leg-target`) at the height `explore-y` of where the leg began, the feet's own kept within y 52 to 90 (`explore-band`: over the lava sea at y 31 and its lakes, under the roof), so a tunnel in the bedrock climbs (a target more than 3 above sends the steps up first) and one that drifted into the roof comes down.
- **A leg is over** (`leg-over?`) when its end is within reach by the ground, when the world ends two blocks ahead (`open-ahead?`, `look-ahead`: a real server shows 96 blocks, so only the model's captured Nether ends in front of a tunnel, and a leg that cannot go the way it turns tries the next heading), when more tunnels were spent on it than its length asks (a wall it cannot go round), or when the feet are farther from where it began than its length and 64 (another trip's).
- **Only a travelled leg grows the spiral** (`leg-done?`, `leg-max`). A leg given up untravelled (stalled, walled, the world's edge) turns at the same length, and no leg is longer than three spacings (480): stalled legs at caverns used to grow the count without the feet ever walking them, so the searches of issues #527 and #537 (R4-26-1, R4-46-1) aimed at targets 16,896 and 1,919 blocks away and swept one strip for the whole window. A bot already holding such a search recovers at once: its leg's target is read from the capped length, and its next given-up leg turns at that length.
- **A leg that gains no ground is given up** (`leg-progress`, `stall-tunnels`, `stall-gain`). Sim world R2/12 (issue #338, traced on probe/cavern-trace): the first leg east ran into a cavern sixty blocks across and thirty deep (air at every height from y 47 to 59, no roof), and the tunnel zigzagged along its rim, each step a refused air floor ahead and a side step to the z toward the target, which flips at the target's z: 25 steps and 45 digs a minute for 3 blocks of net progress a minute (11 digs a block against 2 in rock, R1/35's 33 blocks a minute), for fourteen minutes; the next leg, south, did the same inside a rock island the cavern surrounds, for fourteen more, eight pickaxes of sticks gone. A look of the planner that is not `stall-gain` (8 blocks; a tunnel in rock gains 32) nearer the leg's end than the best so far counts as stalled, and `stall-tunnels` (3, about four minutes) in a row end the leg. The next leg turns right or left, whichever has the longer `rock-run` (cells ahead, up to `rock-look`, with floor, feet and head solid), so a leg that stalled at a cavern turns to rock it can dig, then back, then on again.
- **A stalled leg bridges a cavern over ground** (`bridge-over-void?`, `:intent/bridge?` set by the planner once `:search/stalled` is above 0): the level step is floored by a block laid from the block under the feet (the step plan's floor op, the body stepping onto it only once it is there), when `bridge?` holds, `ground-below?` finds solid ground within 96 blocks under the new floor (`bridge-ground-depth`, from 48: R2-2-1 and R2-22-2 stood 49 and 37 over a lava sea whose floor lay deeper than that, found no ground to bridge to and pillared 28 blocks up past their target) (never water or the end of the loaded world; lava between only at health 18, the Nether's lava sea under its caves, never hurt), the filler reserve is 16 and health 14. A bridge being crossed (`on-bridge?`: a block under the feet, air under that) keeps the flag when its progress ends the stall mid-span. Sim world R2/12 (issue #338): the cavern is sixty blocks across and thirty deep over basalt; no step round its rim crossed it. Round 3 tried a lane of rock aside first (the end moved sideways so the side steps follow the wall one way): it fired at minute 47, the bot spent six minutes on diagonal and back steps along the receding wall and gained 15 blocks, so it was dropped.
- **A search already running is kept** while its leg goes on; with none (the first look, a death, a failed tunnel that forgot it) the first leg starts east at the feet.
- **Measured, 4 seeds a world, to the fortress in 330 simulated minutes** (probe runs on the commit of the issue 338 branch): R4/33 4 of 4 (54, 54, 57, 248 min), R3/13 4 of 4, R3/21 4 of 4 (85 to 87), R1/43 3 of 4 (112), against none of the 23 runs on main. R2/4 and R2/5 stay 0 of 8: no brick lies within 300 blocks of their landings (the nearest 310 and 337, `capture_nether`'s bricks), a spiral takes hours to reach one, and its cavern floors pace a ledge for half an hour at a time (every step ahead an air floor, refused).
- **What did not help** (same worlds and seeds). Bridging an air floor (the step plan already lays a floor; `safe?` refused an air one) over solid ground within two blocks: bridging every step wandered a cavern, laid 500 blocks in 170 minutes and failed `:not-held`; bridging only toward the target with 24 filler kept still left R3/13 and R3/21 at 1 of 8 and R1/43 at 1 of 4 (its pickaxe worn out, no filler, `:not-held`; a floor in a fungus or wart cell, a cell the server keeps, failed `:rejected` until `blocks/replaceable?` limited it). Ending a leg after three looks that gained under four blocks along its heading, with netherrack, basalt and blackstone as floor filler and no bridging: R4/33 4 of 4 and R3 8 of 8 as before (a few minutes sooner), R1/43 1 of 4, no gain for the added rules. All were taken out; the cavern ledge pacing stays open.

## Never beside lava
`safe?` refuses a step when a cell it opens or its floor is not loaded, a cell it opens is lava or a solid a tunnel never opens (obsidian, bedrock, a spawner: the blaze rods come from it, issue #262), a cell it opens has lava beside it (other than the floor, which is filled first), or the floor is neither solid nor lava a placed block can fill. A passable portal cell needs no dig and may be walked through; its frame remains protected. An air floor is never safe to enter: live run f1-5 stood at a cave's edge, slid off while placing one and fell forty blocks into the lava sea. A cell behind a wall cannot be capped before the wall is dug, and Nether lava flows a block every half second, so instead of capping (ruststeve's `nether_tunnel_step`) the tunnel goes round: `moves` tries the axis with farther to go, up or down first when the target is more than 3 off, then level, then the other axis, sideways, and finally backward.

Solid does not mean walkable: issue #284's two real snapshots chose soul-sand floors, which the route classifies as awkward, and failed the entry walk `:no-path`. Each step first digs a floor unless `[:walk-floor floor]` (`path/floor?`) already holds, then places cobblestone before entering. The dig's guard also holds after placement, so the repair cannot undo itself. A repair is safe only with replacement support, no protected block, and no fluid exposed by the dig. An in-flight step re-reads these ops too: its failed entry walk is dropped when the floor repair becomes the next op; an unsafe repair drops the step instead, choosing again from the current world.

The later R1/6 reproduction of #284 stood on soul sand while its repaired destination was already walkable. The [[pathfinder]] snapped the departure to an isolated gravel neighbour and returned no route. It now searches from the actual supported, clear feet cell without allowing awkward floors as waypoints. A tunnel with an already failed, empty-route `:no-path` entry drops that child only when a route now exists, retaining the step and stopping controls before retrying; an entry still unreachable, or a found route that could not be followed, remains a failure.

Issue #339's R1/5 and R2/23 snapshots opened gravel beds below lava, then repeatedly cleared the gravel falling into the headroom until lava reached the bot and no escape footing remained. `safe?` also checks `intent/fluid-over-falling?` and `intent/bed-over-fluid?` for every opened cell: a fluid-bearing roof or neighbouring bed must stay sealed. Before each body-cell dig and entry walk, as before a floor repair, the tunnel rechecks safety and drops an unsafe step and its child with controls stopped, so an in-flight step replans instead of finishing a stale dig.

Sim race 39113082582's R3/33 reproduction had lava beside the gravel higher up, not directly over it. `intent/fluid-over-falling?` now checks every face of the falling stack, including its sides: both a new step and an in-flight dig or entry leave that bed sealed and choose another way. Dry falling roofs remain allowed, and a non-falling roof still seals fluid above it.

Issue #286 exposed two false dead ends: R3/10 had a safe retreat behind the feet that `moves` omitted, and the real portal at [256 97 464] was passable but rejected. Merely adding those moves paced between the portal's two cells. `next-move` prefers cells not yet left by this tunnel (`:intent/tunnel-visited`). Before retreating it can floor a level turn onto adjoining ground: the air floor must have immediate placement support and another solid horizontal neighbour besides the floor under the feet, and `stairs/over-fluid?` must find no fluid below it. The step must satisfy the same lava and optional water rules with that floor filled. Controls stop when the step is chosen, and its ordinary floor op must be confirmed before the walk begins; this is a short corner onto ground, not permission to enter an air floor or extend a causeway over lava.

A dead end over a drop is neither: issue #338's R3-6-1 (sim race 39593212933, world R3/6) stood on two blocks of a ledge at y 39, seven over the lava sea, every way round them cave air, and no filler of a turn may be laid over fluid. The retreat stepped back into the other ledge cell, and from there the first allowed step was the first cell again: each step was counted (`:intent/taken`), 32 made a tunnel done in fifteen seconds, the next tunnel began with an empty `:intent/tunnel-visited`, and the leg's tunnel quota turned the spiral on the spot, so the bot paced the two cells from minute 90 to the timeout at 330 (1,134 intents). Two things changed. `next-move` now climbs before it retreats (`climb-out?`): an up step whose floor, the cell beside the feet at their own height, is air with a solid face to lay against (`build/foundation` is the cell itself: the ledge's rock, a wall, a block laid before), the block under the feet solid, a filler carried, and the step `safe?` once that floor is laid; the step plan's floor op lays it from the stand, so the body never leaves its footing over the drop, and it is one block against a ledge, not a causeway. And a retreat is counted (`retreat?`: the step goes into a cell the tunnel already stood in, which only the last resort does; `:intent/retreats`, reset by a fresh step): more than `retreats-allowed` (1) in a row is a pace, and the tunnel fails `:blocked` instead of counting the pace as steps. A step that lessens the vertical gap to the target (`gaining?`) is not one of those retreats. While the target is more than `climb` above the feet, `next-move` also takes that climb, including back into a cell already stood in, before any downward safe step: issue #526's R2-29-1 and R2-36-1 held a stone pickaxe and hundreds of cobblestone on a cobble stair over a 2x3 obsidian pad, the level tread beside them already stood in, so the only fresh safe step was down the stair onto the pad, and from the pad every fresh step was air and the tunnel failed `:blocked`. It is still one block laid against a face from the stand, not a floor laid in the air. R2-12-1 (world R2/12) of the same reports was no pace: it tunnelled north through a basalt delta by hand, 7.5 s a block of blackstone, 15 s a step, its pickaxe worn out with one stick and one plank left (no wood in the Nether); that is a supply problem of the portal kit, left open.

The later R4/12 and R4/4 reproductions of #286 were a different dead end: the [[server-model]] placed the arrival on a single gravel block suspended over lava, where no portal frame could fit. The model now requires the frame's footprint before choosing a natural arrival. The tunnel still refuses those unsupported steps; its lava rules are not relaxed to escape an impossible arrival.

A real arrival can be possible and still ring the stand with lava: issue #286's `real-5-s1-Clj_r11` came through a portal cast into a one-deep lava lake, lava north and south of it and obsidian east and west, and `safe?` refused every step at every tick, `:blocked` fifty milliseconds apart. A block placed into a source or a flow replaces it and nothing is dug, so no lava runs into the tunnel; `next-move` now takes, after a fresh step, a floored turn and a climb-out and before a retreat, a step once the lava beside the cells it opens is capped (`sealed?`: some lava, `sealable?` every cell of it has a solid neighbour to place against and a filler is carried for all of them, at most `max-seals`, and `safe-but-lava?`, `safe?` with the lava clause left out, holds). The step plan then starts with the placements (`seal-ops`, step `:seal`, worked from the feet), recomputed from the world each tick, so a cell capped is not capped again and `safe?` holds by the time the first dig runs.

Issue #284's R1-32-2 and R3-17-1 failed `:no-path` three times in a row from a different cause: a step down or up carried the centre half a block past the edge of the block under it, the body held by that edge alone and the cell under the feet air. A walk has no route from a cell with no floor, so the entry walk of every step chosen from there failed. The step was dropped as `off-step?` and chosen again from where the feet were. `recentre` (a perched feet cell, `perched?`) now comes before `next-move`: a level step onto a standable cell beside the feet whose floor is solid, never counted as a retreat.

Issue #284 came back (R1-26-1, sim race 39718481292): `recentre` chose the right level step, east onto the ledge's block, but the walk of that step failed `:no-path` three times. The pathfinder began from the first standable neighbour of the floorless feet cell, a block across a one-wide gap to the west, not the block the box rested on ([[pathfinder]], `path/rests`). The route now starts on the block under the box, so the recentring walk is found and the step lands centred.

Issue #410's R3-38-1 dug a soul sand floor (walkable only as a route's last resort, so replaced) with no filler left and failed `:not-held` three times: `safe?` now asks for a filler wherever a step lays a block (a replaced floor, a lava floor), so a tunnel with none fails `:blocked` rather than reaching the place op, and `stairs/fillers` ends with the Nether's own rock a tunnel digs and carries: soul soil, blackstone, basalt.

## The planner across dimensions
A row with `:goal/dimension` is chosen only there (`plan/here?`). When no row can be chosen here but one is undone elsewhere (`elsewhere?`), the plan waits `:other-dimension` instead of ending, so `--until fortress` in the overworld does not report done, and a run toward `[:nether :fortress]` works the portal, then the fortress.

After a chained run has reached `:nether`, a death can respawn it in the overworld while Nether targets remain undone (issue #287). The always row `:return-nether` works the existing `:nether` build and its supply planner there. Its `:nether-access?` predicate checks the unfinished Nether targets in play, not merely the historical entry milestone: it does nothing before the first entry, in another dimension, or after those targets are reached. Milestones remain historical; a standalone fortress trial does not acquire a portal-building prerequisite.

## The way back
A cell no fresh step leaves used to cost one retreat, then a pace, then `:blocked` (three of them fail the plan): sim snapshots R3-5-2 (a cavern's rim, a pillar of laid blocks over the void) and R4-11-1 (a pocket dug down into the bedrock, lava ringing it) stood in such cells with the branch that went on a few cells back never tried. The world keeps a trail for the target (`:fortress/trail`, `trail-of`: the feet cells steps began from, oldest first, and the cells found to lead nowhere), across the tunnels of one target (another target starts a new one). A fresh step pushes the cell it leaves; with no fresh step, the step back to the trail's last cell (`step-back`: one step away and `safe?`) is taken, that cell dropped and the one left marked dead, and `next-move` is given the trail and the dead cells as visited, so a dead end is entered once and a branch explored from the cell it began at. A step back is no retreat (`:intent/retreats` stays 0); with an empty trail, or a last cell not a step away, the retreat count of before stands.
