# Ender pearls

Twelve eyes of ender need twelve pearls. The roadmap (#10) first chose bartering; issue #263 reversed it: a stack of gold for twelve pearls is a mine the run never makes, so the bot hunts endermen.

## What the jar says (probe runs 38889277228, 38890566495)
- **Loot.** `loot_table/entities/enderman.json`: one roll of `ender_pearl`, `set_count` uniform 0 to 1, then `enchanted_count_increase` uniform 0 to 1 a level of looting. A pearl one kill in two: about twenty-four kills for twelve.
- **Where.** `worldgen/biome/warped_forest.json`: the only monster spawner is the enderman (weight 1, groups of four; spawn costs charge 1.0, budget 0.12). Crimson forest spawns zombified piglins, hoglins and piglins instead.
- **The enderman.** 40 health, 7 attack (Normal), speed 0.3, follow 64 (`EnderMan.createAttributes`). `isBeingStaredBy` is `isLookingAtMe` with 0.025 and its eye height (`mob/stared?` is the same cone and line). `EndermanLookForPlayerGoal`: an angry one stared at within 4 blocks (distance squared 16) teleports away; one farther than 16 (256) teleports toward its target after 30 ticks. `hurtServer`: a projectile or a potion sends it away; a hit whose source is a living entity never does, so the bot's sword never makes it teleport.

## The rows
`:ender-pearls` (`--until pearls-12`, provides 12 `ender_pearl`) and `:ender-pearl` (`--until pearls-1`, the gym's first trial, one pearl), both done by `:provided?` and planned by `:pearls`, priority 19. Both are asides for now (`plan/aside?`): no chained run takes them and `plan/current-until` stays at the fortress until the stages before them are proven; raising the chain is its own change.

## The :pearls plan (pearls.clj)
Out of the burrow's end every look but a provoke's is turned from a calm enderman's eyes (`plan/steer :pearls`, `look-off`: straight down at the same yaw, as the dragon row does; a walk steers by yaw and a dig or a place is judged by distance), and any running intent but an answer (`answers-to-hand`: a fight, a back-off, a meal, a fire dug out) or the walk home ends with an angry enderman, blacklisted or not, within `strike-range` (`beset?`, `plan/interrupts? :pearls`), so the hunt plans again: home, the fight held there, or the fight where it stands. Sim probes 39887735653 and 39887752302 (seeds 4 and 5): four bots stared at one two or three blocks off on a walk or a tunnel out of the burrow, and the intent went on while it struck them from 20 health to none in six to ten seconds. With its pickaxe spent on burrows (`worn-out?`: a `:burrow` done this run, `memory/last-done`, and none held), no burrow near and no angry one near (`menaced?`), the plan waits `:no-pickaxe`, which fails it: no burrow can be cut, and sim probe 39890964793 (seed 5) struck calm endermen in the open in worlds R2 and R3 after its pickaxe broke and was slain, R2 nine times; a bot that never held one hunts in the open as before. Held fights skip one given up on (blacklisted) unless it is within the sword's reach, and no unheld fight starts inside the burrow: sim probe 39890029962 (worlds R1 and R4, seed 3) held three fights in a row on one by the mouth that never came, each `:unreachable`, and the plan failed.

In order:

1. **The kill's drop.** Never with another angry enderman (blacklisted or not) within `danger-radius` of the feet and `danger-rise` of their height (`menaced?`): the hunt meets it first (sim probe 39885574495, world R2 seed 5: two provoked at once, one killed, and the walk to its pearl was slain beside it by the other). `fresh-kill`: the last intent a `:fight` for `:pearls`, done, with `:intent/target-pos`, its target gone (dead or removed; a fight broken off under 10 health is done too, with its enderman alive, and is no kill). A pearl lying round where it was last seen (`drop-of`: `drops/nearest`, a drop holding a pearl or not yet saying what it holds) is picked up by #262's `:collect-drops`; the block an enderman carried drops beside its pearl and is left, since a drop now says what it holds.
2. **The drop still to come.** While the body still lies there (`body?`: the entity still tracked, dead), `{:plan/wait :drop}`: the server drops the loot as it dies and removes the body a second later.
3. **The hunt** (`hunt`), over the nearest enderman within `hunt-radius` 32 (`at-hand`: the nearest not blacklisted when one is within `strike-range`, else the nearest of all, blacklisted or not: one a held fight gave up on is still angry, and batch pearls-warped-b's bots walked out to it and were slain, while batch pearls-warped7-a's run 4 had three in one spot 3.8 off, two blacklisted, and cut no burrow for the third; at hand when within `strike-range` 4.5 by the distance in three, never on the plane alone: batch pearls-warped-b's run 3 held a fight twenty minutes on one two blocks off on the plane and fourteen below, batch pearls-warped-c's run 1 stood over one fourteen below for forty) and the nearest of them that is coming (`entity/creepy?`: the server's creepy flag, metadata index 17, set while it has a target):
   - out of the burrow (`shelter`: `:pearls/shelter` within `shelter-reach` 16, still a `shelter?` and not given up, `stale?`) with one coming, one within `strike-range` 4.5, under 10 health, or another hostile within the `:defend` row's `defend-radius` 4 (`threat`: every hostile kind but the enderman and the creeper is the hunt's while a burrow is near, `combat/answering :pearls`, so the `:defend` row leaves it; gym batch pearls-warped4-a's runs 3 and 4 were slain by mobs that row fought in the open at the forest's edge, each with a burrow cut, and batch pearls-warped4-b's arena run 2 by the zombies, skeleton and creeper of the dark round the arena): a walk back to its end (`:intent/for :shelter`: the walk ends in that very cell, as a build's stand does). Batch pearls-263b: one bot met an enderman in the open after a provoke that ended by its coming near, and another backed away hurt; both died;
